MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time volumetric clouds, god-rays (crepuscular rays), dense smoke/fire plumes, and 3DGS / NeRF volume compositing
  • Explaining why Forward-Euler opacity alpha = sigma_t * ds explodes and flips transmittance negative whenever optical depth tau > 1
  • Using the exact intra-step integral (1 - exp(-sigma_t * ds)) so coarse ray-march steps remain unconditionally bounded in [0, L_e]
  • Cancelling sigma_t in (sigma_s / sigma_t) * (1 - exp(-sigma_t * ds)) to avoid 0/0 singularities in empty air (sigma_t -> 0)

⛔ Not for

  • Opaque hard-surface mesh rendering where rays terminate immediately at the first surface hit without participating media
  • Highly heterogeneous multi-scattering clouds without shadow-map / phase-function evaluation at each sample point
  • End-to-end neural radiance field training pipelines (Instant-NGP / Mip-NeRF 360)
  • Single-layer 2D UI sprite alpha blending where optical thickness is already pre-baked into [0, 1]
