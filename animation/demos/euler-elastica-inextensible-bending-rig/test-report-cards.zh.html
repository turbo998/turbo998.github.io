MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Real-time GPU grass blades, wheat fields, bamboo stalks, hair strands, and fur bending under strong wind
  • Cantilever fishing rods, radio whips, and robotic continuum arms requiring exact rest-length preservation
  • Retracting vertical coordinate y = s * cos(theta) alongside horizontal sway x = s * sin(theta) (or circular arc sin(k*s)/k, (1-cos(k*s))/k)
  • Eliminating the 2x rubber-band growth artifact when grass blades bend to 60 degrees

⛔ 不适合

  • Highly extensible rubber bungee cords that are physically intended to stretch to 2x-3x their rest length
  • Static 2D flat UI buttons
  • Rigid stone walls with zero bending deflection
  • 1D audio gain sliders
