MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Batch-generating 4K seamless tileable PBR materials (BaseColor, Normal, ORM, 16-bit Height) for Unreal Engine 5 / Unity 6 open-world biomes and virtual production stages
  • Generating 8x8 (64-frame) seamlessly looping 2D/2.5D VFX flipbook sprite sheets (fire, smoke, magic portals, water splashes) with premultiplied alpha and motion-vector maps
  • Hardware-compressed KTX2 / BC7 / ASTC asset delivery with zero UV border seams or baked directional shadows

⛔ 不适合

  • Plugging raw zero-padded Midjourney/Flux RGB images directly into UE5 BaseColor slots without de-lighting or circular border wrapping
  • Extracting non-looping video frames with black/white background halos into particle flipbooks
  • Static text-only database schemas
