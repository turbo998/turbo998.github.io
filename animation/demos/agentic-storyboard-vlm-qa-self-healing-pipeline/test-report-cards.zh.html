MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Unmanned overnight e-commerce & short-drama AIGC factories requiring >99.5% anatomical/prop accuracy without human QA bottlenecks
  • Isolating <6% localized hand, finger, cup-handle, or gibberish-sign defects via GroundingDINO + SAM-2 while freezing 94% of approved pixels
  • Enforcing a 2-hop automated inpainting circuit breaker before escalating <0.5% edge cases to a 人工复核-in-the-Loop Dead-Letter Queue

⛔ 不适合

  • Discarding a 94%-perfect generation to fix a 6th finger via global random seed rerolling
  • Publishing raw batch AIGC posters directly to ad networks without VLM anatomical and OCR Levenshtein verification
  • Unbounded agent retry loops with no max-hop circuit breaker
