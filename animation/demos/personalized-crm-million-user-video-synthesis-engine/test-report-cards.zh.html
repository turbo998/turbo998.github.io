MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Airline, banking, telecom, gaming, and e-commerce Year-in-Review ("Wrapped") personalized videos for 1,000,000 to 10,000,000 members
  • Combining 95% pre-baked Closed-GOP AI persona B-roll segments with 5% (90-frame) dynamic variable slot NVENC encoding and <12ms fMP4/HLS byte-stream splicing
  • Lazy Just-in-Time (JIT) synthesis triggered only when a user opens the push notification (~18%~25% open rate), saving 75%+ of idle pre-generation compute

⛔ 不适合

  • Re-encoding all 1,800 frames of a 60s video per user when 1,710 frames are shared across cohorts
  • Pre-rendering 10,000,000 MP4 files upfront for dormant accounts that never open the campaign message
  • Static plain-text SMS notifications
