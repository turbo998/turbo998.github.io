MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time ray-traced reflections in UE5 Lumen, Unity HDRP, Blender Cycles, and WebGPU/Three.js path tracers
  • Stretching the GGX ellipsoid to the unit sphere to sample only camera-visible normals (V.H > 0)
  • Canceling (V.H) / ((N.V)*G1(V)) in the PDF so estimator weights never exceed 1.0000 at grazing angles
  • Eliminating the 1.5000x at 60° and 3.3828x at 80° firefly variance spikes of full-NDF D(H)*(N.H) sampling

⛔ Not for

  • Purely analytic direct-light point evaluations without Monte Carlo importance sampling
  • Perfect delta mirrors with alpha == 0 where reflect(-V, N) requires only 1 deterministic ray
  • Lambertian diffuse hemisphere cosine sampling
  • Unlit 2D sprites
