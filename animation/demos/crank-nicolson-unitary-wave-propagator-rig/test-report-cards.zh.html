MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Shallow-water ripple heightfields, acoustic wave propagation, and modal sound/vibration synthesis requiring unconditional stability AND zero artificial damping (Crank & Nicolson 1947)
  • Quantum / Schrödinger and dispersive water-wave (i*omega) time-stepping where |G(theta)| === 1 is mandatory
  • Vorticity-preserving fluid advection and lively cloth/hair secondary oscillations
  • 1-step rational Cayley update replacing 64-substep explicit RK4 marching

⛔ 不适合

  • Purely diffusive heat equations du/dt = +nu * laplacian(u) where high-frequency L-stable damping at theta -> infinity is desired
  • Static unmoving meshes
  • 2D UI button hover states
  • Nearest-neighbor texture copies
