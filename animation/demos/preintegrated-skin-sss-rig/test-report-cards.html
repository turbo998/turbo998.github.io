MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time digital human faces, ears, noses, fingers, wax candles, and translucent jade props on mobile/WebGL2/VR
  • Explaining why longer red mean free path (ℓ_R=1.2mm vs ℓ_B=0.2mm) produces a 5.75× crimson warmth band across the θ=90° shadow terminator
  • Parameterized 2D (N·L, κ) pre-integrated skin LUTs driven by screen-space curvature κ = ‖∇N‖ / ‖∇P‖
  • Replacing 256-tap numerical diffusion integrals or 6-pass texture-space blurs with O(1) closed-form pre-integration

⛔ Not for

  • Large flat skin regions with high-frequency shadow maps where Separable Screen-Space SSS (Jiménez SSSSS) is needed
  • Opaque metallic or stone surfaces with zero subsurface mean free path (ℓ = 0)
  • Neural portrait relighting or diffusion-based face synthesis models
  • Thin back-lit ear pinnae transilluminated from behind (requires thickness map transmittance exp(-σ_t d))
