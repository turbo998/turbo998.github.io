MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Converting static webtoons, manga chapters, and graphic novels into 9:16 vertical animated short dramas and motion comics across 12 languages
  • Stripping speech balloons and SFX via Manga-OCR + Line-Art Inpainting before any camera or character animation
  • Routing 70% dialogue panels to 2.5D 3-plane parallax + 5-vowel mouth sprites ($0.002/shot) and 30% combat climaxes to panel-anchored FLF2V

⛔ Not for

  • Feeding raw comic panels with speech bubbles still attached directly into Image-to-Video models (bubbles float and melt across faces)
  • Using unconstrained 3D realistic video diffusion on 2D cel-shaded manga panels (destroys original artist linework)
  • Text-only novels with zero existing comic artwork
