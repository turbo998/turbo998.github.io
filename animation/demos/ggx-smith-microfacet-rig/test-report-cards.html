MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time WebGL2 / WebGPU PBR metallic-roughness shaders and 3DGS specular relighting lobes
  • Explaining why omitting Smith G2 causes 1/(4*mu_l*mu_v) to explode into a radioactive white rim at grazing angles
  • Algebraically canceling 2*mu_l*mu_v in G2 with 4*mu_l*mu_v in the denominator into the singularity-free V(mu_l,mu_v,a)
  • Replacing trigonometric tan^2(acos(mu)) in Smith Lambda with purely algebraic sqrt(alpha^2 + (1-alpha^2)*mu^2)

⛔ Not for

  • Pure Lambertian matte diffuse surfaces where view-angle microfacet specular masking does not apply
  • Multiple-scattering rough conductor energy compensation (Kulla-Conty LUT) at alpha > 0.8
  • Wave-optics diffraction grating iridescence requiring full spectral path tracing
  • Neural intrinsic image decomposition (DiffusionRenderer) on uncalibrated 2D photographs
