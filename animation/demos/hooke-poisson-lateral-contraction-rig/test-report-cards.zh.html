MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Rubber band and slingshot elastic cords, muscular tendon/biceps elongation, stretchy cartoon limbs, and octopus tentacle vertex shaders
  • Scaling transverse cross-section radius by lambda_z^(-nu) = 1 / sqrt(lambda_z) for nu = 0.5 elastomers so stretched bands naturally neck down and conserve volume
  • Supporting arbitrary material Poisson ratios nu in [0, 0.5] via Hencky power law R_perp = R_0 * lambda_z^(-nu)

⛔ 不适合

  • Honeycomb cork stoppers where Poisson ratio nu is approximately 0.0
  • Auxetic metamaterials with negative Poisson ratio (nu < 0) that fatten when stretched
  • Static UI text labels
