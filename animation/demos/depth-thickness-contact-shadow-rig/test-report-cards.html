MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Screen-Space Contact Shadows (SSCS), Screen-Space Reflections (SSR), and Horizon-Based Ambient Occlusion (HBAO / GTAO) thickness rejection
  • Explaining why a single-layer 2.5D depth buffer treats thin foreground objects as infinite pillars extending to +infinity unless gated by thickness
  • Linearizing Reversed-Z depth buffers via a single scalar division z = z_near / d_rev before evaluating metric thickness intervals
  • Testing |z_ray - (z_front + 0.5*tau)| <= 0.5*tau in a single branchless centered comparison

⛔ Not for

  • Off-screen occluders outside the camera frustum or hidden behind a nearer foreground layer (requires shadow maps or ray-traced BVH)
  • Multi-layer deep shadow maps or hardware ray-traced shadows that already store exact back-face geometry
  • Neural screen-space ambient occlusion / shading networks (DeepAO / NNAO)
  • Unlit 2D orthographic UI layers with no depth buffer
