精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise-wide AIGC marketing portals serving 500+ internal marketers and external ad agencies under strict brand VI and legal governance
  • Pre-flight Prompt AST compilation (injecting Pantone/Hex palettes, studio LUTs, and IP blocklists) paired with post-flight CLIP trademark scanning and C2PA v2.1 signing
  • Maintaining immutable WORM audit logs for enterprise copyright indemnity across Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex AI, and self-hosted ComfyUI pipelines

⛔ 不适合

  • Unmoderated consumer meme generators with no brand identity or legal compliance requirements
  • Offline compiler optimization for C++ kernels
  • Raw network packet switching
