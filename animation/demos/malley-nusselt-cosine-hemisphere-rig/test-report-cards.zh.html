MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Lambertian diffuse bounce sampling & ambient occlusion (AO) ray generation in real-time and offline path tracers (Malley 1988 / PBRT)
  • Cancelling the cos(theta)/pi rendering equation term in the PDF to achieve zero white-furnace variance
  • Projecting Shirley concentric or Vogel spiral 2D disk samples vertically onto the 3D hemisphere via z = sqrt(1 - r^2)
  • Closed-form algebraic lift replacing 64-ring polar trigonometric CDF inversion

⛔ 不适合

  • Isotropic volumetric phase functions over the full 4*pi sphere where cos(theta) is not present in the integrand
  • Mirror-like delta specular reflections
  • Flat 2D UI layouts
  • 1D audio white-noise generators
