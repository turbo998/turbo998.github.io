MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Physical camera glare/starburst post-processing, space telescope starlight shaders, optical microscopy, and quadrature CoC + Airy depth-of-field pipelines
  • Expanding diffraction Airy disk radius linearly with f-number r_Airy = 1.2197 * lambda * N so stopping down to f/16 or f/22 naturally blooms point highlights and softens micro-contrast
  • Combining geometric defocus circle of confusion and wave diffraction in quadrature r_total = sqrt(r_geom^2 + r_Airy^2)

⛔ Not for

  • Wide-open fast lenses (f/1.4) on out-of-focus backgrounds where geometric bokeh circle of confusion is 50x larger than the Airy disk
  • Unlit wireframe CAD viewports
  • Audio equalizer bars
