MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Camera stop holds, character foot-plant height curves (y >= 0), and bounded opacity/exposure ramps
  • Explaining why central-difference tangents m_k=(y_{k+1}-y_{k-1})/2 ring across flat plateaus and step changes
  • Horner-factored ((a*s + b)*s + c)*s + d cubic evaluation without Math.pow or basis array allocations
  • Keyframed 4D Gaussian Splatting opacity/scale tracks where negative undershoot or ceiling overshoot breaks rendering

⛔ Not for

  • Intentional cartoon anticipation and follow-through bounce where artists desire overshoot beyond keyframes
  • C^2 curvature-continuous aerodynamic fairings where zeroing tangents at extrema is undesirable
  • Spatial 3D flight loops where constant-speed arc-length reparameterization is the primary constraint
  • Optical-flow neural video frame interpolation (RIFE / FILM) operating on raw pixel grids
