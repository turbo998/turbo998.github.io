MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Dielectric PBR shaders for porcelain, plastic, ceramic, skin, and painted surfaces (Ashikhmin-Shirley JGT 2000 / UE5 / Filament)
  • Eliminating the glowing radioactive 200% white rim on bright dielectric silhouettes at grazing angles (mu -> 0)
  • Single-pass forward & deferred lighting where diffuse albedo must scale by (1 - F) before adding specular
  • 1-subtraction (1 - F) coupling replacing 64-bounce subsurface Stokes series summation

⛔ Not for

  • Pure bare conductors (metals with metallic = 1.0) where transmitted diffuse is identically zero at all angles
  • Intentional stylized unlit rim-light outlines in cartoon shaders
  • Flat 2D vector icons
  • 1D audio pan laws
