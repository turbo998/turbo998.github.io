MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • SDF central-difference gradient reflections and raymarching bounce rays without inversesqrt(dot(N,N))
  • Elastic particle-wall collision bounce shaders where ||v_out|| === ||v_in|| must hold under scaled normals
  • Interpolated vertex normals and blended triplanar/detail normal maps before cubemap reflection lookup
  • Zero-sqrt rational division 2.0 * dot(N, I) / dot(N, N) replacing normalize(N) + reflect(I, N)

⛔ Not for

  • Diffuse lighting terms that still require dot(N, L) / sqrt(dot(N, N)) linear cosine normalization
  • Static 2D UI layout
  • Unlit flat wireframe rendering
  • 1D audio gain sliders
