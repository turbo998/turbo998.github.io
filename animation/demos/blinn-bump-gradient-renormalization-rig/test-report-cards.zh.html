MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Procedural heightfield bump shaders, screen-space dFdx/dFdy surface gradients (Mikkelsen 2020), and ocean wave normals
  • Applying hardware inversesqrt(1.0 + gx*gx + gy*gy) after subtracting tangent surface gradients from N_0
  • Locking normal length at 1.0000x (e.g. (0.60, 0.80) at g=0.75) instead of inflating dot(N, L) and dot(N, H) by 1.25x-1.80x
  • Replacing 64-substep Frenet arc rotations with 1 hardware rsqrt instruction

⛔ 不适合

  • Flat unperturbed geometry with zero height gradient (g = 0)
  • Unlit wireframe or flat-color debug shaders
  • 2D UI layouts
  • Audio buffer mixing
