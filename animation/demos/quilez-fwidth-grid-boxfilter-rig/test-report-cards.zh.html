MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Procedural CAD ground planes, architectural tile joints, synthwave horizon grids, and Shadertoy checkerboards
  • Dividing line thickness T by the hardware pixel footprint w = fwidth(u) = |du/dx| + |du/dy| when w > T
  • Fading sub-pixel horizon lines smoothly by C = T/w (0.2500 at 4T, 0.0769 at 13T, 0.0625 at 16T) to lock total flux at 1.0000x
  • Replacing 64-tap sub-pixel supersampling with 1 hardware fwidth division per pixel

⛔ 不适合

  • Pre-baked bitmap textures that already use hardware trilinear/anisotropic mipmap chains
  • Screen-space UI lines aligned to integer pixel coordinates with w == 1 px
  • Volumetric fog density fields
  • Audio FFT spectrograms
