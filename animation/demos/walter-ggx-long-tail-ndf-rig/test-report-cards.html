MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time & offline PBR metals, chrome, car paint, and wet surfaces matching MERL measured specular halos (Walter EGSR 2007 / Burley Disney 2012)
  • Preserving both a sharp specular core (s->0) and a soft wide metallic bloom tail (s in [1.5, 4.0]) with a single roughness parameter alpha
  • Rational 1/(1+s^2)^2 evaluation requiring zero transcendental exp() instructions
  • Closed-form Cauchy-Lorentz kernel replacing 64-substep Lorentzian ODE integration

⛔ Not for

  • Purely diffuse matte surfaces with zero specular lobe
  • Replacing multi-scattering Kulla-Conty energy compensation at extreme roughness alpha > 0.8
  • Flat 2D vector typography
  • 1D audio low-pass filters
