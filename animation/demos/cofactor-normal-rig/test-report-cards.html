MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Squash-and-stretch character animation, elastic soft-body rigs, and non-uniformly scaled 3D CAD assemblies
  • Explaining why normals transform as covariant 1-forms (cotangent vectors) via (M^-1)^T rather than tangent vectors M*t
  • Division-free 3x3 cofactor column cross-product formula cof(M) = [c1×c2, c2×c0, c0×c1] inside vertex shaders
  • Deforming 3D Gaussian Splatting covariance ellipsoids and local shading frames under elastoplastic gradients F

⛔ Not for

  • Pure rigid-body rotations and uniform scaling where M^T M = s^2 I makes M*n and cof(M)*n colinear
  • Neural diffusion video relighting (IC-Light / RelightVid) operating on 2D latent pixel grids
  • Full precomputed radiance transfer (PRT) spherical-harmonic triple-product relighting
  • Topological mesh tearing or fracture where surface tangents are undefined
