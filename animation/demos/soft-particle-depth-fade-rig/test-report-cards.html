MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Ground-hugging smoke, explosion fireballs, waterfall mist, dust clouds, and shockwave billboards in UE5, Unity, and Three.js
  • Eliminating razor-sharp 2D polygon intersection lines where billboard quads slice into sloped terrain or walls
  • Guaranteeing C1 zero-derivative opacity contact at Δz = 0 and Δz = d_fade via cubic Hermite s²(3 − 2s)
  • Replacing 64-step per-particle volumetric ray marching with a 1-tap scene depth buffer lookup

⛔ Not for

  • Opaque rigid debris particles (rocks, shrapnel, shell casings) that should intersect or collide sharply
  • Pure forward pipelines without a readable depth texture / depth prepass
  • Neural 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) or NeRF training
  • Camera near-plane clipping (requires separate near-plane distance fade)
