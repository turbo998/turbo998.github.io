MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Sub-pixel hair strands, animal fur, grass blades, brushed wires, and textile yarns shaded from curve tangents T (Kajiya & Kay SIGGRAPH 1989)
  • Cylindrical specular cone foundation sin(theta_L)*sin(theta_V) - cos(theta_L)*cos(theta_V) requiring exact sin(theta) radicals
  • GPU hair cards and Alembic/USD groom curve pipelines where 64-sided cylinder tessellation per hair is prohibitive
  • 1-sqrt closed-form cylindrical integral replacing 64-slice azimuthal circumference quadrature

⛔ Not for

  • Flat wide macroscopic ribbons whose width exceeds several pixels and has a single planar surface normal
  • Unlit wireframe debug lines
  • Isotropic point particles
  • 1D audio gain envelopes
