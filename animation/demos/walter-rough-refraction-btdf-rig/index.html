Walter IOR-Weighted Half-Vector & Jacobian Law

H_t = −normalize(η_i · V + η_o · L), J_t = η_o² · |L·H_t| / (η_i(V·H_t) + η_o(L·H_t))²

For a ray refracting from θ_i = 60° in air to θ_t = 35.26° in glass, weighting V and L by (1.0, 1.5) cancels their tangential components (1.0·sin 60° − 1.5·sin 35.26° = 0), preventing the -68.06% darkening of unweighted bisectors.
