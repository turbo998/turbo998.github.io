MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Frosted glass, etched crystal, ice cubes, and translucent polymers in glTF KHR_materials_transmission, Blender, and Three.js
  • Weighting incident and transmitted vectors by (eta_i, eta_o) so H_t = -normalize(eta_i*V + eta_o*L) satisfies Snell law
  • Eliminating the 12.37° half-vector tilt error and -68.06% at 60° / -83.64% at 80° transmission darkening of unweighted bisectors
  • Pairing with the Walter Refraction Jacobian eta_o^2*|L.H_t| / (eta_i*(V.H_t) + eta_o*(L.H_t))^2

⛔ Not for

  • Opaque metallic mirrors where reflection eta_i == eta_o makes the unweighted bisector exact
  • Zero-thickness flat decals without refraction IOR jump
  • Highly turbid multiple-scattering milk or skin (use Christensen-Burley BSSRDF Round 108)
  • Unlit wireframe overlays
