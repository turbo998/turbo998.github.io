MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise fashion e-commerce 5,000-SKU seasonal lookbook factories, multi-size/multi-ethnicity virtual model try-on, and catwalk video synthesis
  • Conditioning IDM-VTON / CatVTON on 24-part DensePose UV body coordinates, SCHP clothing-agnostic masks, and high-res flat-lay garment encoders
  • Restoring pixel-locked foreground hand/finger and face/hair alpha masks over the warped garment to eliminate 6-finger diffusion artifacts

⛔ 不适合

  • Rigid boxed electronics background replacement (use Alpha-Lock + IC-Light instead)
  • Plain text document OCR
  • Audio-only podcast mastering
