MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Post-process object and camera motion blur in UE5, Unity HDRP, Call of Duty, and DOOM (TileMax + 3×3 NeighborMax)
  • Explaining why blurring along v_center(x) = 0 on background pixels scissor-clips 100% of the exterior motion trail
  • Dilating dominant velocity across K×K tiles via separable TileMax and 3×3 NeighborMax passes before bilateral depth gathering
  • Replacing 64-tap brute-force neighborhood velocity searches with O(1) NeighborMax tile lookups

⛔ Not for

  • Static architectural renders with zero camera or object velocity (v = 0 everywhere)
  • Curved spinning propeller blades requiring rotational angular-velocity polar blur
  • Neural optical-flow video frame interpolation (RIFE / DLSS 3 Frame Generation)
  • 2D UI text overlays that must remain sharp during camera pans
