MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Texture atlas tiling (fract(U)), spherical equirectangular longitude seams (atan2(z,x)), and ray-traced / ray-marched procedural surfaces
  • Explaining why GPU 2×2 pixel-quad dFdx(fract(U)) subtracts 0.0625 − 0.9375 = −0.8750 (7.00× spike) and jumps +2.81 mip levels
  • Passing unwrapped continuous derivatives ∂U/∂x, ∂U/∂y directly to textureGrad() to eliminate 2-pixel-wide blurry seam lines
  • Replacing 4-ray supersampled finite differences with closed-form analytic ray-differential Jacobians

⛔ Not for

  • Unwrapped monotonic UV meshes without atlas tiles, fract(), or atan2() branch cuts
  • Unfiltered nearest-neighbor pixel art textures with mipmapping disabled (GL_NEAREST)
  • Neural single-image super-resolution (ESRGAN / DLSS) upscaling models
  • Vertex-only gouraud shading without texture sampling or normal reconstruction
