MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Brand keynote product-generation evolution morphs (Gen-1 -> Gen-5 hardware), architectural interior raw-to-furnished reveals, and CAD wireframe-to-photo materializations
  • Daisy-chaining multi-shot long takes where Shot k End Keyframe is bit-identical to Shot k+1 Start Keyframe (Wan 2.2 FLF2V / Kling 2.1 Start-End / Veo 3 First-Last Frame)
  • Coupling 6D Plücker camera ray trajectories with optical-flow midpoint structural guidance for smooth whip-pan match cuts

⛔ 不适合

  • Open-ended ambient background loops with no required target end pose (standard single-frame I2V suffices)
  • Static typography-only slides with no camera or object morphing
  • Audio-only voiceover tracks
