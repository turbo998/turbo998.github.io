MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise CTO/SA architecture reviews selecting across Sora 2, Veo 3, Kling 2.1, Seedance 1.0 Pro, Runway Gen-4, LTX-2, Wan 2.2 14B, and Remotion
  • Calculating the exact monthly volume break-even point (~18,500 5s clips/mo) where self-hosted 8xH100 Wan 2.2 FP8 beats commercial per-second APIs
  • Evaluating effective cost per approved usable second (accounting for seed reroll multipliers R_fail) and IP indemnification coverage

⛔ 不适合

  • Binding all enterprise video workloads (including drafts, data charts, and high-volume SKU ads) to a single $0.50/s proprietary API
  • Spinning up dedicated 24x7 H100 clusters when total enterprise demand is under 2,000 clips per month
  • Using non-commercial research-only checkpoints for commercial brand campaigns
