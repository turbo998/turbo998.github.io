MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Pixar OpenSubdiv, Blender, Maya, and GPU hardware tessellation of character faces, creature shoulders, and CAD fillets
  • Projecting valence-n extraordinary star-poles directly onto P_infinity in O(1) time without recursive mesh blowup
  • Eliminating +0.5000m star-pole spikes (at n=7) and LOD popping when hair follicles, buttons, or decals attach to subdivision cages
  • Pairing with De Casteljau Cubic Subdivision (Round 58) and Barycentric Displaced Micro-Meshes (Round 57)

⛔ Not for

  • Pure triangle-mesh Loop subdivision surfaces (which use Loop beta-weights instead of Catmull-Clark (n, 4, 1) quad weights)
  • Infinitely sharp creased corners tagged with crease sharpness s -> infinity (which intentionally stay at V0)
  • Un-subdivided flat low-poly retro props
  • Neural point-cloud surface reconstruction
