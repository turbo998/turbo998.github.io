MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Hardware/software Z-buffer scanline interpolation, bilateral depth upsampling, and Hi-Z reprojection where 1/z is affine in screen space (Blinn 1992)
  • Screen-space ray marching (SSR / SSGI / contact shadows) and deferred decal world-position reconstruction
  • Tile-based depth plane equations a*x_s + b*y_s + c = 1/z for depth-buffer compression
  • 1-reciprocal affine 1/z lerp replacing 64-step 3D view-ray plane root-实测跨越斜墙屏幕插值参数 t ∈ [0.0000, 1.0000]（z₀ = 1.0 m → z₁ = 9.0 m）的 17 个站点：Blinn 倒数深度线性插值（1/z = lerp(1/z₀, 1/z₁, t)）在全部 17 个站点恒定锁死深度精度比 R = 1.0000×（中点精确落于调和均值 1.8000 m，0/17 弯曲），而朴素线性视图深度插值在第 4 站鼓包至 2.3333×（3.00m vs 1.29m）、在第 8 站中点高达 2.7778×（5.00m vs 1.80m，+177.78%）、在第 12 站达 2.3333×（7.00m vs 3.00m），污染全部 15/17 个内部站点。

⛔ 不适合

  • Orthographic parallel projections where view-space z is already linear in screen space
  • 2D flat UI layers at constant depth
  • 1D audio fade curves
  • Non-projective world-space voxel grids
