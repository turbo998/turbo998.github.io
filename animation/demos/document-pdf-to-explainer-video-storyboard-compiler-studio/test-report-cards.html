MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Turning 50–120 page enterprise technical whitepapers, RFP bid responses, and quarterly analyst reports into 3-minute executive summary videos (16:9 & 9:16)
  • Parsing PDFs via Docling/MinerU into typed JSON ASTs, reflowing dense A4 tables into 28px animated Remotion/HyperFrames SVG charts with exact source-page citations
  • Upgrading audio-only NotebookLM-style dual-host podcasts with synchronized visual evidence cards and spotlight page zooms

⛔ Not for

  • Pasting raw 9pt A4 PDF pages directly into a 16:9 video frame where mobile viewers cannot read a single word
  • Asking text-to-video diffusion models to render financial tables or benchmark bar charts from text prompts (100% numeric hallucination)
  • Unstructured handwritten sticky notes with no tabular or hierarchical content
