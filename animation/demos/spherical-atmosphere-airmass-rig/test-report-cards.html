MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time procedural sky, sunset transmittance, and aerial perspective in UE5 SkyAtmosphere, Frostbite, and Flight Simulator
  • Explaining why flat-earth planar secant 1/cos(theta) diverges to infinity at theta=90 deg and turns the horizon pitch black
  • Bounding grazing horizon optical airmass to sqrt(2*R_p/H + 1) = 39.89 atmospheres via exact quadratic ray-sphere intersection
  • Replacing 64-step 3D atmospheric transmittance ray marching with a 4-op closed-form spherical chord formula

⛔ Not for

  • Overhead noon-only lighting models where solar zenith angle never exceeds 45 deg (m_sph ≈ m_flat within 0.06%)
  • Indoor windowless dungeon scenes with zero sky or atmospheric scattering
  • Neural LDR-to-HDR skybox diffusion or panorama outpainting models
  • Non-spherical flat tabletop dioramas without planetary curvature
