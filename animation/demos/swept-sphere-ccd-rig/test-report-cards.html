MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Fast projectiles, pinball physics, cloth-vs-body collision, and high-velocity rigid bodies against thin walls
  • Explaining why discrete end-of-step collision tunnels whenever v * dt > 2 * (R_sphere + h_wall)
  • Closed-form Minkowski sum expansion (shifting sphere radius R onto the static obstacle slab) for O(1) TOI ray-casting
  • Sub-frame Time-of-Impact (TOI tau*) remainder velocity integration x_next = x_hit + (1 - tau*) * v_reflected * dt

⛔ Not for

  • Quasi-static resting stacks where displacement per frame is much smaller than contact skin thickness
  • Deformable continuum fracture or fluid splashes requiring Material Point Method (MPM / PhysGaussian) grids
  • Rotating non-convex mesh-vs-mesh continuous collision without conservative advancement
  • Generative video world models (Cosmos / Sora) that synthesize pixel frames without explicit rigid-body kinematics
