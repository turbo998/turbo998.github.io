MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Raymarching (Sphere Tracing) of ellipsoids, capsules, petals, and non-uniformly scaled SDF primitives without overstep tunneling (Quilez 2019 / Hart 1989)
  • SDF soft shadows, ambient occlusion, and collision thickness probes requiring isotropic Euclidean distance bounds around thin axes
  • Closed-form dual-norm ratio k0*(k0-1)/k1 avoiding 6th-degree polynomial root 实测 17 个椭球短轴压缩比站点（κ = 1/r_z ∈ [1.00, 5.00]）：Quilez 双范数梯度归一化 SDF（k₀(k₀−1)/k₁）在全部 17 个站点恒定锁死单位利普希茨步长比 L = 1.0000×（0/17 超步穿模），而朴素非均匀缩放 ‖p/r‖−1 使步长比随 κ 线性爆炸，在第 4 站（2:1）达 2.0000×、第 8 站（3:1）达 3.0000×、第 12 站（4:1）达 4.0000×、第 16 站（5:1）暴冲至 5.0000×（污染全部 16/17 个非球形站点）。
  • 2-norm algebraic evaluation replacing 64-iteration Newton-Lagrange closest-point solvers

⛔ 不适合

  • Uniformly scaled spheres (r_x = r_y = r_z = R) where length(p) - R is already an exact unit-gradient SDF
  • Explicit triangle-mesh rasterization
  • Flat 2D CSS borders
  • 1D audio pitch shifting
