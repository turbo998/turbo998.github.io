MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Giant mech/kaiju footstep ground subsidence, soft mattress/sofa cushion indentation, and heavy vehicle terrain heightmap shaders
  • Applying the Boussinesq 1/r half-space Green kernel w(r) = w_0 / sqrt(1 + (r/r_c)^2) so surrounding ground slopes smoothly toward the load center instead of punching cookie-cutter step cliffs
  • Superposing multiple contact loads F_i on a GPU heightmap in O(1) per contact via linear elasticity

⛔ Not for

  • Dry cohesionless sand avalanches governed by Coulomb angle-of-repose plastic slumping
  • Unloaded skybox domes
  • 2D text editors
