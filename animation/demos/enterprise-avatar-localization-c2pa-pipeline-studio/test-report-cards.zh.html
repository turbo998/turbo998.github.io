MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise global L&D training video factories, multilingual product onboarding tutorials, and executive town-hall localization across 17+ languages
  • Isolating neural phoneme-viseme lip-sync to the speaker facial ROI while compositing translated PPT/code slides via crisp Remotion vector tracks
  • Enforcing cryptographic C2PA v2.1 provenance manifests and Biometric Speaker Consent Vault authorization to prevent unauthorized executive deepfakes

⛔ 不适合

  • Unconsented voice/face cloning without cryptographic authorization tokens
  • Single-language raw camera recordings requiring zero localization or slide overlays
  • Pure offline CFD fluid simulations
