MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • FMCG, cosmetics, luxury tea/wine gift boxes, and electronics packaging pipelines requiring both 3D e-commerce hero renders and 300-DPI print-ready CMYK+Spot PDFs
  • Generating AIGC illustrations directly on flat FEFCO/ECMA 2D CAD dielines with 3mm bleed and cross-crease continuity
  • Compiling vector hot-foil, blind-emboss, and spot-UV varnish layers into both offset printing spot plates and Blender/Three.js PBR Metallic/Normal/Clearcoat maps

⛔ Not for

  • Generating perspective 3D box photos via raw T2I prompts when the factory requires a flat unfolded 300-DPI CMYK dieline
  • Letting diffusion models rasterize EAN-13/UPC barcodes or FDA/GB nutritional tables
  • Unpackaged digital-only software downloads
