MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Hardware/compute-shader Displaced Micro-Meshes (DMM), Nanite-style micro-polygon displacement, and ray-traced terrain/masonry silhouettes
  • Explaining why fixed-step Parallax Occlusion Mapping (POM) tunnels through narrow ridges and slices grazing silhouettes into pancakes
  • Using a regular 2^L dyadic barycentric grid so micro-vertices are addressed implicitly without storing index buffers
  • Solving ray-microsegment intersection in O(1) via t* = (y_ray - H_k) / (H_{k+1} - H_k) without iterative secant/bisection steps

⛔ Not for

  • Flat painted surfaces with zero geometric relief where standard normal mapping suffices
  • Overhanging 3D caves or vector-displaced earlobes that fold back over the base triangle normal (requires 3D mesh or vector displacement)
  • Text-to-PBR / heightmap diffusion material generators
  • Distant low-LOD background props smaller than 1 screen pixel
