MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Character foot-plant squash, bouncing rubber balls, and vehicle tire-ground contact patches under variable impact load F
  • Enforcing Hertzian sub-linear contact radius a prop F^(1/3) and area A prop F^(2/3) so heavy impacts raise peak core pressure p_max = 1.5 * F / A instead of flattening like a pancake
  • Driving contact shadow penumbra and pressure-sensitive decal opacity from Boussinesq hemispherical stress p(r) = p_max * sqrt(1 - r^2/a^2)

⛔ 不适合

  • Plastic sheet-metal stamping above yield stress where mean pressure saturates at hardness H and area grows linearly with F
  • Static non-deforming rigid cubes resting on flat tables
  • 1D audio volume sliders
