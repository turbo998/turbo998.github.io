MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Physically based forward/mobile shaders, gloss-map pipelines, and spherical harmonic / cosine-power lobe fitting
  • Scaling peak intensity by (s + 2) / (2*pi) so narrower, smoother highlights grow proportionally brighter at their center
  • Locking integrated hemisphere energy at E = 1.0000 instead of losing 75.00% at s=6 and 80.00% at s=8
  • Bridging legacy shininess assets into energy-conserving PBR pipelines in 1 multiply-add

⛔ Not for

  • Wide-tailed metallic microsurfaces requiring inverse-polynomial GGX/Trowbridge-Reitz tails
  • Purely diffuse Oren-Nayar or Lambertian substrates
  • Unlit UI elements
  • 2D audio waveforms
