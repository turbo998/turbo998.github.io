MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • UE5 Mesh Distance Field Soft Shadows, Shadertoy/WebGPU ray-marched SDF scenes, and global distance-field skylight occlusion
  • Rendering contact-hardening soft shadows (sharp at t → 0, wide penumbra at large t) using only 1 ray march per pixel
  • Tracking min_t(k · d_SDF(P + t·L) / t) at zero extra SDF evaluation cost inside the sphere-tracing loop
  • Replacing noisy 16–64 ray stochastic area-light shadow sampling and temporal denoisers

⛔ Not for

  • Non-SDF triangle soup meshes without precomputed Mesh Distance Fields or analytical SDF primitives
  • Pinhole point lights in vacuum with zero angular radius (k → ∞)
  • Neural screen-space shadow synthesis or spatiotemporal SVGF denoising
  • Translucent colored stained-glass shadows
