MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Open-world terrain cliffs, Marching Cubes voxel caves, procedural SDF rock formations, and architectural ruins without manual UV unwrapping
  • Eliminating 5.76× (80°) to 11.47× (85°) vertical texture streak stretching on steep heightfield slopes
  • Blending orthogonal YZ, XZ, and XY world-plane projections directly from world normal N without inverse trigonometry
  • Pairing with Reoriented Normal Mapping / Whiteout blending (Round 65) for tangent-free normal maps

⛔ Not for

  • Skinned deforming characters where world-space coordinates swim across moving limbs (use rest-pose object space or UV unwrapping)
  • Single flat horizontal floors where 1-tap XZ planar projection is already exact
  • Neural texture synthesis or diffusion inpainting
  • Unique non-tiling hero decals requiring custom 2D UV parameterization
