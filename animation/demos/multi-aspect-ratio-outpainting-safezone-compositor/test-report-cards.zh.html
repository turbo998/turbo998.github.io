MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Omnichannel global ad campaigns adapting one master asset into 16:9 YouTube, 9:16 TikTok/Reels/Shorts, 3:4 Xiaohongshu, 4:5 Instagram, and 21:9 banners
  • Combining SAM-2 saliency bounding-box protection with Flux Fill / Wan 2.2 directional outpainting and Remotion responsive UI dead-zone avoidance
  • Guaranteeing 0% hero SKU truncation and 0% overlap with platform Like/Share/Caption overlays across 17 ad placements

⛔ 不适合

  • Single-aspect-ratio internal cinema screenings with no social or mobile distribution
  • Command-line text log files
  • Raw audio-only radio spots
