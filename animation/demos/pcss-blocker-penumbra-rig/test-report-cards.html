MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Contact-hardening sun, streetlamp, and studio softbox shadows in UE5, Unity HDRP, Three.js, and architectural visualization
  • Keeping character feet and tire contact points razor-sharp (w=0.0000m at d_r=d_b) while smoothly widening foliage/roof shadows with distance
  • Pairing with Vogel Golden-Angle Disk Poisson Sampling (Round 59) and Gruen Sin-Theta Normal-Offset Shadow Bias (Round 87)
  • Computing per-pixel penumbra filter radius in 3 algebraic operations from average blocker depth d_b

⛔ Not for

  • Infinitely distant zero-diameter pinhole point lights where physical penumbra width is identically zero everywhere
  • Shadow maps lacking linear or reversed-Z depth readback for blocker search
  • Volumetric cloud self-shadowing (use Beer-Lambert Extinction Round 55 or SDF Cone Shadows Round 94)
  • Baked static lightmaps with offline path-traced irradiance
