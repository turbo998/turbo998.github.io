MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Procedural roughness remapping, cloud density shaping, and animation easing in WebGL2, WebGPU, MaterialX, and Houdini VEX
  • Replacing expensive transcendental Perlin pow(u, log(a)/log(0.5)) calls with 1 scalar division u / ((1/a - 2)*(1 - u) + 1)
  • Preserving smooth shadow toes (B(0.25) = 0.0769) and full white ceilings (B(1.00) = 1.0000) while hitting B(0.50) = a exactly
  • Eliminating the lower-30% black crush and -30.00% highlight ceiling loss of naive additive offsets

⛔ 不适合

  • Linear identity passthroughs where a = 0.5000 is permanently fixed
  • Non-monotonic wave folding or sinusoidal oscillation
  • Unbounded HDR signals outside [0, 1] before normalization
  • Static integer bitmasks
