MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Large-deformation FEM soft bodies, elastic characters, cloth bending, and vegetation swaying through arbitrary 3D rotations
  • St. Venant-Kirchhoff, Neo-Hookean, and XPBD finite-strain continuum solvers requiring exact SO(3) objectivity
  • Eliminating the 1/cos(theta) linear-FEM ghost inflation artifact when appendages rotate past 30-60 degrees
  • Zero-SVD quadratic strain energy evaluation via C = F^T * F

⛔ 不适合

  • Purely static bridges or buildings with sub-milliradian rotations where infinitesimal strain is valid
  • Static 2D HTML text nodes
  • Unconstrained point-cloud particles without elastic connectivity
  • 1D audio volume sliders
