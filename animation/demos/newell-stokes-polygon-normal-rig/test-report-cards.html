MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • CSG Boolean fracture, Voronoi destruction shards, and CAD n-gons containing collinear T-junction vertices or concave corners (Newell 1975)
  • Area-weighted vertex normal accumulation where ||N_Newell|| directly supplies the exact polygon area weight without triangulation
  • Best-fit plane normal estimation for slightly non-planar quads and subdivided polygons
  • 5-edge boundary contour sum replacing 64-cell interior surface quadrature

⛔ Not for

  • Guaranteed non-degenerate equilateral triangles with no subdivision vertices
  • 2D axis-aligned sprites with fixed (0, 0, 1) normal
  • Point clouds without polygon connectivity
  • 1D audio envelopes
