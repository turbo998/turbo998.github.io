MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Overlapping translucent CAD cutaways, automotive glass assemblies, particle smoke plumes, and instanced foliage/hair where per-triangle sorting is impossible
  • Explaining why decomposing OVER into additive weighted color Σ(αCw(z)) and multiplicative revealage Π(1-α) achieves strict commutativity
  • Using fixed hardware blend states (GL_ONE, GL_ONE and GL_ZERO, GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_COLOR) in a single geometry pass without atomic linked lists
  • Eliminating sudden front-to-back color popping when camera rotation flips bounding-box sort order

⛔ Not for

  • Near-opaque overlapping decals (alpha > 0.95) with conflicting colors where exact per-pixel K-Buffer / Depth Peeling ordering is required
  • Single non-overlapping transparent layers where standard sorted premultiplied OVER is already exact
  • Neural multi-layer alpha matting and image decomposition networks
  • Fully opaque geometry passes using hardware Z-buffer depth writes
