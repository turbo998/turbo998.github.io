MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Deferred-rendering G-buffer 32-bit/16-bit world-space and view-space unit normal compression
  • Meshlet vertex normal streams and 3D Gaussian Splatting covariance/axis quantization
  • Explaining why n_z = +sqrt(1 - n_x^2 - n_y^2) flips all grazing view-space or back-facing world-space normals where n_z < 0
  • Using signNotZero(v) = (v >= 0 ? +1 : -1) so the South Pole (0, 0, -1) unfolds cleanly to a corner instead of collapsing to (0, 0)

⛔ Not for

  • Unnormalized displacement or velocity vectors whose magnitude ||v|| != 1 carries physical speed or height data
  • Tangent-space normal maps strictly guaranteed to have n_z > 0 where BC5 two-channel xy storage suffices without world/view rotation
  • Monocular RGB-to-normal neural estimation networks (DSINE / Marigold)
  • 3D rotation frames requiring twist angle around the normal (use unit quaternions or QTangents instead)
