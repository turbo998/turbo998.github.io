This bench evaluates deterministic Cigolle et al. (2014) L₁ octahedral projection and `signNotZero` lower-hemisphere corner unfolding versus naive positive-Z hemisphere reconstruction and trigonometric spherical angles across 17 meridian stations (θ = 0° to 160°). It does not execute DSINE, Marigold, or neural monocular normal estimators.
