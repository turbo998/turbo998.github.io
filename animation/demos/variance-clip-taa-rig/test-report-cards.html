MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time Temporal Anti-Aliasing (TAA), temporal upscalers (TSR), and spatiotemporal stochastic screen-space reflections/GI
  • Explaining why transforming 3x3 neighborhoods into orthogonal YCoCg space tightens the AABB along the luminance axis compared to RGB
  • Ray-clipping stale history toward the neighborhood mean μ rather than component-wise clamping to preserve chroma hue
  • Single-pass raw moment accumulation (m1, m2) with closed-form L-infinity slab ratio t = 1 / max(1, |H-μ|/(γσ))

⛔ Not for

  • Static offline path-traced renders where true multi-thousand-sample Monte Carlo accumulation requires no history clamping
  • Sub-pixel thin wires or 1-pixel distant sparkles smaller than the 3x3 neighborhood without responsive velocity/translucency masks
  • Neural optical-flow frame generation and super-resolution networks (DLSS 3 / FSR 3)
  • Single-frame spatial anti-aliasing (FXAA / MSAA) with no temporal history buffer
