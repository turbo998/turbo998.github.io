MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Planetary crater, hurricane vortex, and Voronoi cellular noise decals that must stay circular rather than stretching 2x horizontally at 60 deg latitude
  • Rhumb-line (loxodrome) navigation charts and spherical environment projections requiring exact local angle preservation (k_meridian = k_parallel = sec phi)
  • Real-time WebGL/Canvas vertex & fragment shaders needing closed-form Gudermannian UV mapping without 64-step meridional quadrature

⛔ 不适合

  • Global area-budget or radiometric irradiance integration across the whole sphere (use Archimedes cylindrical equal-area z = sin phi instead)
  • Polar caps at phi -> 90 deg where Mercator coordinate y = ln(sec phi + tan phi) diverges logarithmically
  • Static 2D flat UI buttons without spherical coordinates
