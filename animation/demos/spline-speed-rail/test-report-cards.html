MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Constant-speed 3D camera dolly and drone spline paths
  • Pre-normalizing Bezier trajectories before TrajectoryCrafter / 4DGS
  • Eliminating S-curve crawl and straightaway speed surges
  • Fast LUT + Newton parameter inversion in browser loops

⛔ Not for

  • TrajectoryCrafter monocular video diffusion inference
  • Point-cloud depth reprojection and disocclusion inpainting
  • Physical motorized camera slider torque control
  • Discontinuous teleport cuts without a continuous path
MIT original code. Synthetic educational scene; local checks reproduced. No production, physics, or neural inference certification.
