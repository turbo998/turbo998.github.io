MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • 3D microvascular blood-vessel vasodilation, microfluidic lab-on-a-chip networks, plant xylem sap transport, and viscous lava-tube flow animations
  • Scaling mean particle velocity as (R/R_0)^2 and volumetric flux as (R/R_0)^4 under constant pressure gradient Delta_p / L
  • Driving Murray’s law (R_parent^3 = R_1^3 + R_2^3) and realistic parabolic radial velocity profiles v(r) = 2*v_mean*(1 - r^2/R^2)

⛔ 不适合

  • High-Reynolds-number fully turbulent rough-pipe flows (Re > 4000) governed by the Darcy-Weisbach friction factor
  • Ballistic particle beams in vacuum
  • Static 2D UI text
