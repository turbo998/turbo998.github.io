MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Water-surface, ocean riverbed, and wet-asphalt driving shaders with controllable CPL polarizer glare removal
  • Foliage waxy cuticle and glass display-case rendering separating s-polarized specular glare from diffuse albedo
  • 自动放行nomous driving perception simulations modeling polarizing camera filters at Brewster incidence (~53 deg)
  • Separate R_s and R_p Fresnel components modulated by Malus analyzer weight cos^2(phi_pol)

⛔ 不适合

  • Normal incidence (theta_i = 0 deg) where s- and p-polarizations are degenerate (R_s === R_p)
  • Purely metallic conductor reflections where R_p does not vanish at a real Brewster angle
  • Static 2D UI layout
  • 1D audio gain sliders
