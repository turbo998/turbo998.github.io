MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D camera CPL (Circular Polarizer) filter rotation, polarized sunglasses cutting Brewster-angle water/windshield glare, and LCD pixel shutter dimming shaders
  • Evaluating exact Stokes/Jones cosine-squared transmittance I(phi) = I_0 * cos^2(phi) = I_0 * (e_pol . e_analyzer)^2
  • Revealing underwater fish, riverbed stones, and car interiors by extinguishing s-polarized surface glare

⛔ Not for

  • Purely unpolarized thermal blackbody emission with no polarizer in the optical path
  • Non-optical audio filters
  • Static 2D UI text
