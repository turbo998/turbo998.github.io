MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Indoor rooms, corridors, showrooms, and underground garages using Box/Sphere Reflection Captures in UE5, Unity, and Three.js
  • Anchoring wall, window, and pillar reflections to exact floor corners without camera-dependent sliding
  • Evaluating ray-AABB slab intersection t_box = min((B_max − P)/R) in 6 ALU instructions per pixel
  • Blending multiple local reflection probes with world-space distance weights

⛔ Not for

  • Distant outdoor sky clouds, mountains, and stars where r → ∞ is physically accurate (infinite skybox)
  • Complex non-convex interior furniture self-reflections (combine PCC with McGuire homogeneous SSR from Round 85)
  • Neural radiance caching (NRC) or 3D Gaussian Splatting novel-view synthesis
  • Purely diffuse surfaces with zero specular reflectance
MIT original code. Synthetic educational scene; local checks reproduced. No production, physics, or neural inference certification.
