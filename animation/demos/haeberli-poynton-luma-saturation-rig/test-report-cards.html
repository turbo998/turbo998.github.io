MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Linear-space HDR post-processing saturation, vibrance, and grayscale transitions without brightness pumping
  • Tone-mapping chroma-preservation passes that blend per-channel tonemapped RGB with luminance-mapped RGB
  • WCAG contrast-preserving UI desaturation and disabled-state material shaders
  • Single-dot-product Y = dot(rgb, vec3(0.2126, 0.7152, 0.0722)) before mix(rgb, vec3(Y), u)

⛔ Not for

  • Multi-channel non-visual data textures (such as XYZ normal maps or metallic-roughness-AO masks)
  • Static 2D wireframe geometry
  • Purely monochrome single-channel depth buffers
  • 1D audio gain sliders
