MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Large-scale city digital twins, flight simulators, and planetary 3D scenes with z_near = 0.1m and z_far -> infinity
  • Explaining why subtracting z_near/z from 1.0 in Forward-Z destroys float32 exponent dynamic range
  • Zero-cost Reversed-Z projection matrix with GREATER_OR_EQUAL depth test and 0.0 clear value in WebGPU / WebGL2
  • Eliminating fragment-shader gl_FragDepth logarithmic depth writes that break hardware Early-Z culling

⛔ Not for

  • Fixed-point integer depth buffers (DEPTH24_STENCIL8) where ULP spacing is constant across [0, 1]
  • Legacy OpenGL [-1, +1] clip-space pipelines without glClipControl(GL_ZERO_TO_ONE) or EXT_clip_control
  • Orthographic shadow cascades where depth is already linear in view-space z
  • Monocular RGB-to-depth neural foundation models (Depth Anything V2 / Marigold)
