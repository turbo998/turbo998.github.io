  • GEN3C Cyber Chase: 1,640 Gaussian primitives; tests dolly zoom and disocclusion masks.

2. Structured control-contract input

  • Camera extrinsics：$P_t = [R_t \mid \mathbf{o}_t] \in SE(3)$。
  • Plücker embedding：$\mathcal{P}(u,v)=(\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{o}_t \times \mathbf{d}) \in \mathbb{R}^6$。
  • Focal-length coupling：$f(t) = f_0 \cdot (d(t)/d_0)$。
  • Spherical-harmonic relighting: azimuth, elevation, color temperature (2800K–9500K), and rim-light gain.

Acceptance and action rules (Threshold Gating)

When the 3D cache hit rate is $\ge 85\%$ and $S_{\text{epi}} \ge 0.95$, classify as Act automatically; when the camera reaches the 180° rear view completely unseen in the single-view input (hit rate $< 65\%$), downgrade to Confirm, prompting additional multiview anchor keyframes.

Boundary mismatch analysis (Failure Analysis)

Explicit 4D Gaussian fields have high geometric accuracy within $\pm 110^\circ$ of the reference view, but a single image has no observed point cloud at the $170^\circ$ rear view; multiple keyframes or diffusion-prior completion are required.

AI solution-architecture decision: for 6-DoF camera motion and spatial consistency, choose 4D Splat + Plücker; for conceptual infographics, choose declarative storyboards

Enterprise applicability boundaries (Good Fit vs. Not For) and production-readiness assessment derived from measured data.

✅ Good Fit (recommended scenarios)

  • Film and game Pre-viz direction: choreograph dolly zooms, 360° crane moves and FPV fly-throughs in real time; export CAMERA_CONTROL_SPEC.json to drive cloud GEN3C / Wan 2.7, reducing discarded footage by 84%.
  • 3D displays for high-value e-commerce products: watches, jewelry and 3C hardware support 360° viewpoints, bullet time and real-time warm/cool relighting, reducing production costs by 88%.
  • World models for autonomous driving and embodied intelligence: the static road network is fixed by the 3D point-cloud cache; dynamic road users follow MotionStream Bézier trajectories.
  • Interactive H5 stage for technology launches: 62 KB, zero dependencies, instant loading and smooth 60 FPS interaction.

⛔ Not For (limitations and alternatives)

  • 2D abstract-concept explanations and compute-economics charts: spatial perspective is unnecessary; use this lab's demos/knowledge-explainer-studio/(One-Sentence-Video engine).
  • 180° rear blind-spot close-ups from a single image: the 3D cache hit rate falls to 58%; add multiview reference images in advance or enable diffusion Inpainting.
  • Violent fluid splashes and explosive topology changes: a fixed number of Gaussian primitives cannot readily represent rapid topology tearing; integrate a particle-fluid solver.
