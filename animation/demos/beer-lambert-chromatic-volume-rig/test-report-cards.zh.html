MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Ocean water, colored glass, gemstones, honey, and wine in glTF KHR_materials_volume, OpenPBR, Blender, and Three.js
  • Shifting hue naturally from crystal-clear thin edges (S=1.00x at 0m) to cyan shallows (2.00x at 1m) and deep sapphire (8.00x-16.00x at 3-4m)
  • Eliminating the muddy thin rims and washed-out deep interiors of single-channel scalar alpha tinting
  • Evaluating exp(-sigma_RGB * d) analytically in 1 vector instruction instead of 64-step volume raymarching

⛔ 不适合

  • Opaque metallic or painted surfaces with zero interior transmission
  • Highly scattering milk or skin dominated by subsurface dipole diffusion (use Christensen-Burley Round 108)
  • Thin soap bubbles governed by wave interference (use Belcour-Barla Iridescence Round 102)
  • Flat 2D UI icons
