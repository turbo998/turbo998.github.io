This bench evaluates deterministic algebraic Bishop Parallel Transport Frames (Rotation Minimizing Frames) via minimal dihedral reflection versus classical Frenet-Serret curvature frames across a 3D S-curve inflection point. It does not execute Gen3C, TrajectoryCrafter, or neural camera motion diffusion models.
