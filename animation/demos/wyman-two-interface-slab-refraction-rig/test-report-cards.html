MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Architectural glass windows, car windshields, cockpit canopies, and ice slabs in glTF KHR_materials_transmission, Three.js, and UE5
  • Refracting across both entry (air->glass) and exit (glass->air) interfaces so background skyboxes preserve 1:1 scale
  • Applying bounded lateral screen-space UV shift Delta_x = d*sin(theta_i - theta_t)/cos(theta_t) (0.5125 m at 60 deg)
  • Eliminating the 24.74 deg (60 deg) and 38.96 deg (80 deg) fish-eye background magnification of single-interface refract()

⛔ Not for

  • Underwater cameras submerged inside an infinite half-space of water where there is no second exit interface
  • Opaque metallic mirrors
  • Purely diffuse matte surfaces
  • 2D UI text
