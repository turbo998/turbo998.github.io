MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Receding asphalt highways, airport runways, tiled floors, and terrain textures viewed at grazing angles (McCormack et al. SIGGRAPH 1999 Feline)
  • Custom virtual texturing (SVT) and ray-cone / ray-differential texture LOD shaders requiring exact minor-axis singular value sigma_minor
  • Procedural stripes, lane markings, and PBR normal/roughness maps where isotropic mip over-blur destroys transverse contrast
  • 1-pass closed-form first-fundamental-form SVD replacing 64-angle polar footprint scanning

⛔ 不适合

  • Purely isotropic billboard sprites always facing the camera at A = 1.0
  • Untextured flat-shaded vertex colors
  • 1D audio lookup tables
  • Point-sampled nearest-neighbor pixel art
