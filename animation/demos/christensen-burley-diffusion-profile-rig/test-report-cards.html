MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Jade, marble statues, wax candles, milk, and human skin in Pixar RenderMan, Blender Cycles/EEVEE, and UE5 Subsurface Profile
  • Combining a sharp near-source cusp (e^(-r/d)) for crisp pores with a wide diffusion tail (e^(-r/3d)) for deep translucency
  • Eliminating the +11.86% near-core wax bloat at 1d and -94.68% deep-tail extinction at 3d of single-Gaussian blurs
  • Direct importance sampling via analytic CDF inversion in 2 exponential terms without dipole Hankel integrals

⛔ Not for

  • Opaque metallic conductors with zero subsurface penetration (use Gulbrandsen Conductor Fresnel Round 107)
  • Optically thin single-sheet foliage without multiple internal scattering
  • Large-scale atmospheric sky Rayleigh/Mie airmass (use Spherical Atmosphere Round 73)
  • Unlit flat UI badges
