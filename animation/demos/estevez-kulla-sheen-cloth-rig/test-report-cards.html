MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Velvet, suede, denim, and peach-fuzz cloth in glTF KHR_materials_sheen, Blender, and Three.js
  • Modeling upright pile fibers peaking at grazing silhouettes (I(80°)=0.9698) with dark centers (I(0°)=0.0000)
  • Eliminating the +1.0000 center plastic hotspot and -66.67% at 60° rim starvation of cosine lobes
  • Evaluating sin^(1/alpha)(theta_h) from 1 - (N·H)^2 without inverse trig

⛔ Not for

  • Flat-woven silk or brushed metal with tangent threads (use Anisotropic GGX Round 99)
  • Smooth dielectric glass, water, or car paint governed by standard surface microfacets
  • Subsurface skin hemoglobin diffusion (use Pre-Integrated Skin SSS Round 66)
  • Unlit flat-shaded sprites
