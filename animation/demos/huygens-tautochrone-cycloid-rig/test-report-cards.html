MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D astronomical clock escapements, kinetic-art wave-pendulum sculptures, large-amplitude swinging censers, and skatepark/rollercoaster cycloid bowl animations
  • Enforcing exact linear arc-length harmonic motion s(t) = s_0 * cos(sqrt(g / 4R) * t) with amplitude-invariant period T = 2*pi*sqrt(4R / g)
  • Modeling cycloidal evolute cheeks that shorten the free suspension cord as L_free = 4R * cos(psi/2)

⛔ Not for

  • Rigid rods that cannot wrap onto cycloidal evolute cheeks
  • Overdamped viscous dashpots with zero oscillation
  • Static 2D UI text
