2 / 7SCENARIO: FLASHINFER ZERO-GRAD SKIP, DSV4.1 TP SHARD & ENGRAMSTORE

On-Device Zero-Grad Compaction Accelerates Log-Prob Backward by 1.943x While DSV4.1 TP4 Sharding Cuts Indexer Work by 75%

Input Case (`B200/GB300 T=65,031 RLHF Log-Prob Backward`, `DSV4.1 TP4 Prefill Indexer` & `DMA-BUF 16 GiB MR`)

RLHF/GRPO post-training batches carry ~50% masked prompt/padding tokens with `dlogp = 0`; replicated DeepSeek-V4.1 dense prefill indexers score every row on every TP rank; and $2^{32}$-aligned $>4\text{ GiB}$ DMA-BUF MRs silently wrap RDMA offsets on Linux `__rdma_block_iter_next`.

Decision 1 · FlashInfer Log-Prob Skip (`#8268`) & MLA 96KiB SMEM Cap (`#8343`) choice · Act automatically

Compact `dlogp != 0` rows on-device without host readback for $T \ge 4097$ (1.943x B200 backward speedup, `234.992 -> 120.949 ms`) and reserve `96 KiB` dynamic SMEM on FP8 page-64 MLA reducers to cap residency at `2` CTAs/SM (1.088x speedup).

Decision 2 · SGLang DSV4.1 TP Shard (`#42968`), Dynamo Sticky (`#15600`) & Mooncake (`#4701/#5113`) score · Act automatically

Shard dense indexer scores across TP4 (-75.0% per-rank work, 1.28x speedup), bind `X-Dynamo-Session-ID` via `dynamo-sticky-min-load` (1.31x locality), and enforce `MC_MAX_MR_SIZE` with `EngramStore` CUDA Graph lookups (1.38x speedup).

Rubin R200 (`sm_107a`) & GB300 (`SM103`): 208-SM `mega_mhc` chain-packing (`#8341`) reaches `1.104x` (`19.248 -> 17.440 us`) and CuTe GB300 `packed-varlen H>=24 -> CLC` (`#2738`) reaches `1.192x` geomean (`1.421x` max).
