2 / 7SCENARIO: DYNAMO 2,048-WORKER ROUTER, TE NVFP4 4-OVER-6 & MOONCAKE IO_URING CLONING

Retaining Prepared Candidate Rows Cuts 2,048-Worker Admission p99 1,316x While Factorized NVFP4 4-over-6 Speeds Up B200 Quantization 2.50x

Input Case (`2,048-Worker Dynamo AgentX Serving`, `B200 8192x8192 NVFP4 4-over-6` & `Mooncake 32 GiB / 385-Ring Cold SSD KV Store`)

Dynamo's scheduler actor discarded caller-prepared candidate rows whenever any rank crossed busy thresholds (`2,764 ms` p99); TE NVFP4 4-over-6 executed 16 slow IEEE divisions per candidate with 8-way SMEM bank conflicts; and Mooncake failed `io_uring_register_buffers` with `errno=14` (`EFAULT`) on `>1 GiB` pools across 385 rings (`4.4 GB/s` `pread` fallback).

Decision 1 · Dynamo Retained Router Rows (`#18009`) & vLLM EPLB Weight Filter (`#64187`) choice · Act automatically

Keep prepared candidate rows and drop only workers whose every rank is full (boosting 2,048-worker saturated throughput 1.396x `7,172 -> 10,013 req/s`, cutting actor CPU -28.15%, and slashing `4.6k req/s` admission p99 1,316.2x `2,764 -> 2.1 ms`) while filtering EP weights by EPLB initial placement (3.659x smaller `384.9 -> 105.2 GiB/rank`).

Decision 2 · TE NVFP4 4-over-6 (`#3643`), Mooncake `io_uring` Clone (`#4788`) & LMCache TP8 Dedup (`#6125`) score · Act automatically

Factorize E2M1 error via $2^k \times \{1, 1.5\}$ with 16-thread row groups (2.50x B200 row+col strict `0.432 -> 0.173 ms`), clone $\le 1\text{ GiB}$ `io_uring` slices across 385 rings (4.773x cold SSD read `4.4 -> 21.0 GB/s`), and deduplicate TP8 MLA KV in LMCache (8.0x reduction `28.0 -> 3.5 MB`).

Additional Verified Gains: PyTorch deterministic `bilinear2d` gather backward (`85.44x` vs old det, `14.19x` vs atomic `#197714`), raw-Philox exponential tails (`0.05x -> 0.99x` at `p=1e-8` `#202464`), and NIXL vectorized descriptor merge (`2.512x` x86 `#2434`).

Architect Playbook for Blackwell/Rubin/MI455X Kernels, 2,048-Worker Dynamo & KV Fabrics

  • FlashInfer 2-slot CuTe KDA barrier (`#8684`), SM120 persistent `vsa_sm120_blk64` (`#8799`), SM107a `<32` K-block `BLOCK_N=256` FP8 GEMM (`#8809`) + 216-SM `K1` wgrad (`#8782`), plain FP32 SIMT router (`#8763`), FA4 2CTA MQA pad (`#3140`), and AITER MI455X `-1` route drop (`#7365`).

  • TE factorized-reciprocal NVFP4 4-over-6 (`#3643`), PyTorch raw-Philox exponential tails (`#202464`) & input-centric deterministic `bilinear2d` (`#197714`), Mooncake cloned `<=1 GiB` `io_uring` (`#4788`), LMCache TP8 dedup (`#6125`), and NIXL vectorized descriptor merge (`#2434`).

⛔ Not For

  • Extending GB300 `H=2048` deterministic-pair RMSNorm backward past `T=58,496` (`#8810` `1.0622x` `dw` breach) or padding `<= 32` dense MQA heads to 2CTA (`#3140`).
  • Rebuilding all 2,048 worker candidate rows on the Dynamo scheduler actor whenever a single rank crosses a busy threshold (`#18009`).
  • Registering `>1 GiB` single `io_uring` buffers per worker ring (`#4788` `EFAULT` fallback to `4.4 GB/s` `pread`) or sampling $p \le 10^{-7}$ tail tokens with legacy FP32-cast `exponential_()` (`#202464`).
