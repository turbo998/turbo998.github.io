PILLAR 3 · NOMAD HEARTBEAT RESILIENCE

Eliminate the 35% false-termination storm caused by silent yamux disconnects

When a non-leader Nomad server silently fails, yamux@v0.1.2 requires 10s–40s to detect disconnection, slower than the default 20–30s heartbeat timeout, causing 7/20 (35%) healthy nodes to be falsely terminated and surviving nodes to overload +53.85%. Harden both layers: (1) configure the Job Group with lost_after="50s", replace=false, reconcile="keep_original"（0 replacements, 10/10 sandboxes and 23/23 NDJSON streams intact); (2) configure the server with heartbeat_grace="50s"（20/20 nodes 100% ready）。

PILLAR 4, 5 & 6 · CLICKUP BRAIN² PATTERN

10x–20x context offloading and scheduled 9 a.m. warming

Following ClickUp (serving 85% of the Fortune 500, SOC 2 & HIPAA): upload large spreadsheets and recordings directly to sandboxes, parse them in code, and return results only, achieving 10x–20x equivalent context expansion; prebake golden templates with 68 tools plus 6 functional templates; combine scheduled warm pools + scale-out at 60% CPU + 15–30 minute Pause/Resume (UFFD+NBD) + HorizonDB checkpoints to absorb the 9 a.m. peak of thousands of sandboxes per minute.
