CARD 07 / 07 · SA DECISION GUIDEGood-Fit / Not-For

Enterprise Solution Architect Fit & Anti-Patterns

Good-Fit 1: High-volume coding, SRE, SQL & data-pipeline agents (>200 runs/day) backed by deterministic tests/compilers.
Good-Fit 2: Model-upgrade transitions (Flash → Gemini 4 Argon/Carbon, GPT-6.2 Sol) where clean hooks outperform bloated prompts.
Not-For 1: Environments with writable grading scripts or non-hermetic sandboxes (guaranteed Mode 5 reward hacking).
Not-For 2: One-off PoCs (<30 runs)—adopt the 5 convergent primitives directly as a starter template without outer-loop search.
