🛡️ 四大隐性成本与护城河总结（【第四种黑猩猩】核心洞察） The Four Hidden Costs & Engineering Moats ("Why Hardware Alone Fails")

01 · The 96% cache-hit rate and traffic advantage

Processing 5.4 billion input tokens/GPU/hour depends on 96% of tokens requiring no GPU compute; they are read directly from the 100 PiB 3FS disks, generating $15.55/hr. Without sustained daily Agent/Chatbot traffic in the hundreds of trillions of tokens, both cache-hit rate and overnight utilization collapse.

02 · Supernodes starting at 128 GPUs and 100 PiB 3FS

DeepInfer supports only NVIDIA SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950; the smallest efficient unit is 64 prefill GPUs + 64 decode supernode GPUs = 128 GPUs, plus per GPU 14.4 TB NVMe storage and 400G RDMA networking: hardware requirements beyond small/medium teams.

03 · Four closed-source balancing layers and 20%+ redundancy cost

Only the offline engine (the F1 engine) is open source; kvcache-store and the four load balancers (Prefill LB, Decode LB, EPLB, Autoscaler) are closed source. Production requires no scale-in at quota utilization >0.8 (at least 20% redundant warm GPUs)and liability for SLA outage compensation.
