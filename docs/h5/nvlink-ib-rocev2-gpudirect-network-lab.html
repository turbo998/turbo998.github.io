🌐 3-Minute Executive Takeaway: Scale-Up vs. Scale-Out, PCIe ACS Traps, ECN<PFC Watermarks & NCCL busbw Math

Golden Rules: (1) Lock TP inside NVLink (900-1800 GB/s) and run DP/PP/EP over 400G/800G RDMA.
