01 / WHY FRONTIER TRANSFORMATION & FRONTIER PARTNER

From Isolated AI Pilots to Continuous Enterprise Value Realization

Most enterprise AI initiatives stall between impressive demos and governed production rollout. Frontier Transformation closes that gap across five connected stages (01 Business Vision → 02 Solution Design → 03 Agentic Engineering → 04 Governed Scale → 05 Value Expansion) and a 5-step Partner Flywheel.

01 · ALIGN

Anchor Vision & Leadership

Commit executive sponsorship, select target industries, and move from scattered tool PoCs to outcome-driven AI transformation.

02 · SKILL

Certify FTE Core Talent

Complete GH-300, AI-103, AB-100 and the 6 required LevelUp hands-on courses (≥80% assessment) to earn the official FTE Badge.

03 · PROVE

Deliver Customer Lighthouse Wins

Deploy cross-stack agents in real customer environments with Secure AI Landing Zones, Microsoft IQ grounding, and HVE delivery.

04 · PACKAGE

Publish #FrontierFTEOffer

Productize repeatable 4–6 week customer-paid discovery & 3-agent go-live sprints in Microsoft Commercial Marketplace.

05 · SCALE

Frontier CoE & Specialization

Institutionalize an internal Frontier Center of Excellence, expand into Managed AI Services, and earn Microsoft AI Specializations.
