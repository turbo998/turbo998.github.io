MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Custom WebGPU/compute-shader Visibility Buffer attribute decoders, software rasterizers, and deferred decal projectors
  • Explaining why 1/w and u/w are affine in screen space while raw u follows a hyperbola under perspective projection
  • Eliminating PS1-style diagonal texture kinking and swimming on perspective-tilted floors and walls
  • Precomputing vertex 1/z_i and u_i/z_i once per triangle so each pixel requires only 2 lerps and 1 scalar reciprocal

⛔ Not for

  • Purely orthographic 2D/CAD projections where w_clip === 1.0 is constant across all vertices (affine interpolation is already exact)
  • Flat screen-aligned 2D UI sprites at constant camera depth z_0 === z_1 === z_2
  • Neural novel-view synthesis or optical-flow warping networks
  • Non-projective spherical fisheye lenses where straight 3D lines do not project to straight 2D screen lines
