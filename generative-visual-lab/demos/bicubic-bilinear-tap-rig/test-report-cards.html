MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • TAA history buffer reprojection in UE5 SampleHistoryCatmullRom, Call of Duty, Frostbite, and Unity HDRP
  • Explaining why 1-tap bilinear history resampling erodes 27.86% of texture contrast after just 8 frames of slow camera pan
  • Merging positive Catmull-Rom weights w1, w2 into shifted hardware bilinear lookups s_12 = w_2/(w_1+w_2) to cut 16 taps down to 5
  • Replacing 16-tap point-sampled tensor-product bicubic loops with hardware-bilinear-merged lookups

⛔ Not for

  • Static screenshots with zero camera or object motion where history reprojection is never shifted sub-texel
  • Unclamped HDR history buffers across high-contrast edges without AABB neighborhood clamping (which would ring)
  • Neural temporal super-resolution models (DLSS / XeSS)
  • Integer texel-aligned copy passes (u = 0.0 everywhere)
