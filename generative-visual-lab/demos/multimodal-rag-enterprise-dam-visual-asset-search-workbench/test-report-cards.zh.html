MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise Digital Asset Management (DAM) platforms managing 100,000 to 1,000,000+ historical live-shoot and AIGC video shots
  • Slicing 60s master videos into atomic 3s shots ([t_start, t_end]) indexed by SigLIP-2 / TwelveLabs Marengo visual embeddings, camera motion, CIE color palette, and C2PA license status
  • Enforcing a <25ms Search-Before-Generate interceptor before dispatching expensive Sora 2 / Veo 3 / Kling 2.1 API calls

⛔ 不适合

  • Storing 60s master videos as unindexed files (`final_v3.mp4`) and relying on manual folder names
  • Re-generating generic B-roll shots via paid video APIs when identical approved shots already exist in the enterprise DAM
  • Single-file personal desktop scratchpads
