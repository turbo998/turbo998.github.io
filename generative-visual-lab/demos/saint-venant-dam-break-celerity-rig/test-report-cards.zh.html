MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Dam-break flood inundation, tsunami shoreline run-up, spilled bucket water spreading, and open-channel sluice gate animations
  • Driving the wet-dry flood front at u_front = 2 * sqrt(g * h_res) and the parabolic rarefaction surface profile h(x,t) = (1/(9g)) * (2*sqrt(g*h_res) - x/t)^2
  • Scaling upstream retreating depression wave speed at -sqrt(g * h_res) while the downstream dry tip advances twice as fast at +2*sqrt(g * h_res)

⛔ 不适合

  • Deep-ocean dispersive Airy capillary-gravity waves where water depth h >> wavelength lambda
  • Confined pressurized pipe water-hammer acoustics
  • Static UI icons
