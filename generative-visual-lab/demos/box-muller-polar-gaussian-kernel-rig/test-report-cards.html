MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 2D Gaussian bloom, stochastic depth-of-field, and Monte Carlo soft-shadow kernel importance sampling (Box & Muller 1958)
  • Subpixel anti-aliasing jitter (2D Gaussian pixel reconstruction filter) where every sample carries equal weight 1/N
  • Subsurface scattering (SSS) Gaussian dipole/multipole radial profile importance sampling
  • 1-log-1-sqrt closed-form polar inverse CDF replacing 64-subring numerical quadrature

⛔ Not for

  • Hard-edged physical circular aperture diaphragms requiring flat top-hat disk density (use Shirley concentric disk there)
  • Unfiltered nearest-neighbor pixel blits
  • 1D linear ramp fades
  • Deterministic box blurs
