MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS / Mip-Splatting) tile rasterizers, point-cloud surface splatting, and anisotropic texture filtering
  • Explaining why convolving a 2D Gaussian splat with a pixel reconstruction filter equals adding lambda = 0.30 px^2 to the diagonal of Sigma_2D
  • Preventing det(Sigma_2D) -> 0 division-by-zero NaNs and sub-pixel needle shimmering on distant or grazing splats
  • Preserving screen-space integrated opacity energy via the analytical determinant factor sqrt(det(Sigma_2D) / det(Sigma_2D + lambda*I))

⛔ Not for

  • Axis-aligned 2D UI billboards with fixed integer pixel sizes that never undergo perspective foreshortening
  • Isotropic point sprites that ignore 3D covariance orientation and blur equally in all directions
  • Offline SfM camera calibration and multi-view 3DGS spherical harmonics optimization
  • Ray-traced triangle meshes that do not project elliptical covariance matrices to screen space
