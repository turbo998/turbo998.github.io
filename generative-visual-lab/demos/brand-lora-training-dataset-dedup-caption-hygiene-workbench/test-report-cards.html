MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Enterprise 3D mascot LoRAs, flagship product SKU consistency LoRAs, and corporate UI illustration style LoRAs on Flux.1-dev and Wan 2.2
  • Pruning >0.92 burst duplicates via DINOv2, masking background loss via BiRefNet alpha, and explicitly captioning variable outfits/backgrounds
  • Selecting the optimal epoch via automated DINO-Identity vs CLIP-Editability Pareto grid evaluation

⛔ Not for

  • Dumping 100 uncurated white-table e-commerce photos with identical generic captions into Rank-128 training
  • Describing core invariant identity traits in captions so the trigger word fails to absorb them
  • Zero-shot general queries that require no custom brand IP
