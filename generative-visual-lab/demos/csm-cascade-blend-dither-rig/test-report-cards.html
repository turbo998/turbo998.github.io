MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Open-world sun Cascaded Shadow Maps in UE5, Unity URP/HDRP, CryEngine, and Three.js CSM
  • Eliminating the visible horizontal shadow resolution seam pushing across roads ahead of the camera
  • Using IGN stochastic single-cascade selection (xi < u^2*(3-2*u)) to get C1 blending at 1x texture cost
  • Pairing with Texel-Snapped Shadow Cascades (Round 52) and Gruen Normal-Offset Bias (Round 87)

⛔ Not for

  • Single-frustum indoor spotlight shadow maps without cascade splits
  • Fully ray-traced hardware shadow rays without shadow map cascades
  • Baked static lightmaps
  • Unlit 2D top-down maps
