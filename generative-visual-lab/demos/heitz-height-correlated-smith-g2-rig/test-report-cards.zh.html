MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Physically based Cook-Torrance & GGX microfacet specular shaders in UE5, Unity HDRP, Frostbite, Filament, and Blender Cycles (Heitz JCGT 2014)
  • Eliminating the spurious +Lambda_v*Lambda_l denominator cross-term that over-darkens rough metals at grazing angles
  • 1-addition denominator 1/(1 + Lambda_v + Lambda_l) that is both more accurate and requires no extra multiplication over separable G1*G1
  • Closed-form height-quantile integral replacing 64-slab microfacet height integration

⛔ 不适合

  • Full multiple-scattering energy recovery at roughness alpha > 0.8 (still requires Kulla-Conty multiscatter lobe)
  • Unlit flat 2D sprites
  • Purely Lambertian diffuse shaders without microfacet masking
  • 1D audio gating
