MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Depth-of-field bokeh aperture sampling, disk area-light shadow rays, and Malley cosine-hemisphere lifting in PBRT, Cycles, and WebGPU
  • Inverting the polar area element dA = r dr dphi with r = sqrt(u) and Shirley concentric wedges phi = (pi/4)*(b/a)
  • Ensuring 25%, 50%, and 75% of samples cover exactly 25%, 50%, and 75% of the lens disk instead of 6.25%, 25.0%, and 56.25%
  • Eliminating the 4.00x central bokeh hot-spot clumping of naive r = u polar sampling

⛔ 不适合

  • 1D linear timeline sampling where Jacobian determinant is already unity
  • Rectangular quad area lights that do not map to a circular disk
  • Pinhole cameras with zero aperture radius
  • Unlit wireframe gizmos
