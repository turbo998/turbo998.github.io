MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • CAD/CAM mechanical cutaways, exploded views, and technical illustrations (Gooch et al. SIGGRAPH 1998) where every bevel in the shadow hemisphere must remain readable
  • Stylized character & cel/ramp shading (Valve Half-Lambert / Team Fortress 2) using u = 0.5*(N*L) + 0.5 to index warm-to-cool color ramps
  • Ink-and-outline NPR renders that reserve darks (<0.20) for crisp black silhouette & crease ink lines
  • 1-instruction FMA evaluation replacing multi-ring environment integration

⛔ Not for

  • Strict zero-bounce darkroom radiometry where physical light cannot wrap behind an opaque terminator
  • Replacing cast shadow maps or screen-space ambient occlusion
  • Unlit UI sprites
  • 1D audio DSP
