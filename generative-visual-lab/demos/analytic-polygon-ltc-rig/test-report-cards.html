MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time architectural windows, virtual-production LED softbox panels, emissive signage, and Linearly Transformed Cosines (LTC) polygonal area lights
  • Explaining why replacing an area panel with a center point light creates an unphysical 1/r^2 singularity when h < panel width
  • Reducing a 2D surface integral over an M-gon to an exact noise-free 1D sum over its M boundary edges via Stokes' theorem
  • Evaluating edge arc-length and normal projection directly from u_k x u_{k+1} without per-edge Gram-Schmidt tangent orthogonalization

⛔ Not for

  • Textured area lights with high-frequency video content without pre-filtered mipmap lookup over the polygon footprint
  • Contact-hardening area-light shadows (umbrae/penumbrae) without an accompanying blocker search or ray-traced visibility pass
  • Single-image diffusion portrait relighting models (IC-Light / DiLightNet)
  • Infinitely distant directional sunlight where solid angle is small and constant across the scene
