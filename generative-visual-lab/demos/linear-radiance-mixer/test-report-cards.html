MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Eliminating dark muddy troughs in complementary cross-fades and particle trails
  • 3D Gaussian Splatting and volumetric alpha compositing in linear radiance
  • Explaining why #FF0000 + #00FFFF at α=0.50 must be #BCBCBC (0.735) instead of #808080 (0.500)
  • Fast CPU/GPU palette interpolation via pre-decoded linear endpoints and OETF LUTs

⛔ Not for

  • HDR-GS dual differentiable CUDA rasterization or GaussHDR neural tone mapping
  • Perceptual hue-wheel palette design where Oklab / Oklch chroma preservation is preferred
  • Un-dithered 8-bit linear framebuffers without sRGB hardware write conversion
  • Spectral rendering with wavelength-dependent dispersion
