MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Grid-based incompressible smoke, fire, and liquid simulators (Stam 1999 Stable Fluids)
  • Colliding plume and obstacle-wake interactions converting compressive inflow into lateral swirling vortices
  • FLIP/PIC/APIC hybrid particle-grid fluid pressure projection steps
  • Preserving exact vorticity curl(u_raw - grad(p)) === curl(u_raw) since curl(grad(p)) === 0

⛔ Not for

  • Supersonic shockwaves where high Mach-number compressibility is explicitly modeled
  • Static 2D UI layout
  • Unanimated static meshes
  • 1D audio gain sliders
