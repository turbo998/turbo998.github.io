MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Pouring water streams, leaky faucet drips, fountain jet breakup, and crown-splash ligament droplet spawning shaders
  • Scaling axial varicose pinch wavelength linearly with local jet radius lambda* = 9.015 * R (k*R = 0.6970) and volume-conserving droplet radius r_drop = 1.89 * R
  • Coupling capillary growth timescale tau = sqrt(rho * R^3 / sigma) / 0.343 to jet radius R^(3/2)

⛔ 不适合

  • Rigid metal pipes or solid cylindrical rods
  • High-Weber-number atomizing fuel sprays governed by aerodynamic Kelvin-Helmholtz stripping
  • Static UI text
