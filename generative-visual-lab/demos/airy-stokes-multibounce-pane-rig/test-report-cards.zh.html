MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Architectural window panes, automotive windshields, and thin-wall glass shaders (glTF KHR_materials_transmission)
  • Soap bubbles, thin ice sheets, and visor shields where front and back interfaces are unresolved in a single mesh layer
  • Grazing-angle glass reflectance smoothly saturating at 2R/(1+R) -> 1.0000 instead of 2R -> 2.0000
  • Closed-form infinite internal bounce summation without recursive ray-tracing loops

⛔ 不适合

  • Thick refractive solid spheres or lenses where front and back surfaces have different normals and refract rays along divergent paths
  • Opaque metallic mirrors
  • Static 2D UI layout
  • 1D audio gain sliders
