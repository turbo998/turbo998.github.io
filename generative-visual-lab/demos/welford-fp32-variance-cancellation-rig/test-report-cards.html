MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • TAA variance-clipping AABB neighborhood passes and SVGF ray-tracing denoiser variance estimation on bright HDR surfaces (Welford 1962 / Knuth)
  • Variance Shadow Maps (VSM) and temporal depth-moment filters at large world-space depth offsets Z_0
  • Single-pass streaming variance accumulation that guarantees non-negative M2 >= 0 without squaring large uncentered values
  • 1-pass Welford update replacing 2-pass 64-sample reference accumulation

⛔ Not for

  • Hardware mipmapped 2-channel VSM textures where moments must be filtered linearly prior to shader readback (use centered depth offset z - z_anchor there)
  • Unlit static 2D sprites
  • Purely deterministic constant-color fills
  • 1D audio volume sliders
