MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Dynamic vehicle reflection probes & omni-directional point-light shadow maps requiring 2 passes instead of 6 cubemap faces (Heidrich & Seidel 1998)
  • View-independent 360-degree environment reflection lookups with zero square-root instructions (1 division by 1 + |R_z|)
  • Eliminating the rear-pole starburst pinch singularity of single-sphere MatCap / Blinn-Newell maps
  • Closed-form rational projection replacing 64-substep geodesic metric evaluation

⛔ 不适合

  • Large low-tessellation triangles spanning the hemisphere equator when using vertex-interpolated paraboloid projection without pixel-shader evaluation
  • Fixed-camera orthographic MatCap sculpting brushes that never access R_z < 0
  • Flat 2D UI sprites
  • 1D audio equalizers
