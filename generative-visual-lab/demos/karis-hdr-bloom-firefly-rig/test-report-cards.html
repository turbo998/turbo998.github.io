MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • First-mip 2×2 HDR downsampling in Jiménez 13-tap / Dual-Kawase bloom pyramids and TAA pre-filtering (UE5, Unity HDRP, Three.js post-processing)
  • Explaining why 1 sub-pixel specular glint (512.0 nits) blows out a linear 2×2 box filter to 128.3750 (128.63× strobe)
  • Bounding arbitrary finite HDR outliers via L_0/(1+L_0) < 1.0 while preserving 100% accuracy on uniform regions
  • Executing single-pass branchless inverse-luminance reduction in registers without temporary weight arrays

⛔ Not for

  • Subsequent lower-resolution bloom pyramid levels (Mip 2..N) where linear energy-preserving tent/Gaussian blurs are desired
  • Radiometric ground-truth total flux integration where every sub-pixel photon must be summed linearly
  • Neural Monte Carlo path-tracing denoisers (OIDN / OptiX / DLSS Ray Reconstruction)
  • LDR 8-bit sRGB UI textures where all pixel values already lie in [0, 1]
