MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Anisotropic geometric specular anti-aliasing (Kaplanyan 2016) on curved cylinders, wires, pipes, and automotive body panels
  • Ray-differential and ray-cone footprint covariance propagation across curved reflectors and refractors
  • Line-drawing hatching (Girshick / Hertzmann) and curvature-driven procedural wear along principal directions (e_1, e_2)
  • Preserving full hoop curvature kappa_1 on cylinders where Gaussian curvature K = 0 and mean curvature H = 0.5*kappa_1

⛔ Not for

  • Purely umbilic spheres where kappa_1 === kappa_2 in every direction
  • Static 2D flat UI buttons
  • Flat planar polygons with zero curvature everywhere
  • 1D audio gain sliders
