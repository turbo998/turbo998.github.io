MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Cobblestone streets, brick walls, relief carvings, and mechanical treads in Unreal Engine, Unity HDRP, and Three.js
  • Eliminating pancake staircase slices on coarse 5-to-16-layer heightfield raymarchers with a single O(1) secant division
  • Locking station 8 (u=0.50) at 0.5000 m and station 12 (u=0.75) at 0.7500 m instead of snapping to 0.6000 m and 0.8000 m
  • Cutting per-pixel texture fetches by 12x compared to 64-layer brute-force heightfield marching

⛔ 不适合

  • Silhouette-extruding geometry at grazing profiles where real mesh tessellation or displacement is required
  • Sharp vertical cliffs with zero horizontal transition where secant interpolation cannot span an undercut overhang
  • Flat painted decals with zero height variation
  • Volumetric clouds or Participating media
