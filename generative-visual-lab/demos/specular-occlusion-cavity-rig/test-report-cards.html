MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Modulating split-sum IBL environment specular reflections in UE5, Frostbite, and Unity HDRP character and vehicle shaders
  • Explaining why applying AO only to diffuse lighting leaves nostrils, wheel wells, and armor crevices glowing with 4.69x sky reflections
  • Tightening specular occlusion below diffuse AO at grazing angles (NdotV = 0.05 -> SO = 0.2131) while relaxing it on smooth mirrors at steep views
  • Replacing 64-step spherical visibility-cone integration with a 4-op closed-form power law

⛔ Not for

  • Direct analytical point or directional lights with explicit shadow maps (which use micro-shadowing rather than ambient SO)
  • Pure diffuse matte plaster with zero specular reflectance
  • Neural screen-space ambient occlusion inference models (DeepAO / Neural GI)
  • Full ray-traced reflection hits where exact geometry visibility is already traced per ray
MIT original code. Synthetic educational scene; local checks reproduced. No production, physics, or neural inference certification.
