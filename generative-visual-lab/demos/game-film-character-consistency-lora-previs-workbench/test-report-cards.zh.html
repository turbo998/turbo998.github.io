MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Game cutscene pre-visualization (Pre-viz), animated film storyboards, and serialized brand mascot video production
  • Combining 4-view Character Turnaround LoRA / IP-Adapter / Kling 2.1 Elements with lightweight 3D mannequin OpenPose & Z-Depth camera blocking
  • Eliminating face/costume identity drift and halving GPU retake waste across 180-degree camera arcs and multi-shot sequences

⛔ 不适合

  • Single standalone wallpaper generation with no recurring character or camera continuity
  • Text-only document summarization
  • Unconditioned random style-exploration moodboards
