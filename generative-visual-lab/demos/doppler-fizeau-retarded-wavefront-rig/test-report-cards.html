MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Moving water-skimmer ripples, swimming waterfowl wakes, sub-sonic acoustic shock rings, and moving radar/sonar pulse visualizations
  • Compressing forward wave crests by (1 - M*cos(theta)) and stretching trailing stern crests by (1 + M*cos(theta))
  • Eliminating the rigid-plastic-bullseye artifact when a ripple emitter translates across a water surface
  • Closed-form directional Doppler wavenumber k(theta) = k_0 / (1 - M * dot(v_hat, r_hat))

⛔ Not for

  • Strictly stationary buoys or fountains with zero horizontal translation velocity (M = 0)
  • Static 2D flat UI cards
  • Unlit wireframe debugging grids
  • 1D audio gain sliders
