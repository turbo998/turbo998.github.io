MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Two-phase GPU-driven cluster and instance occlusion culling in UE5 Nanite, Frostbite, and Unity GPU Resident Drawer
  • Explaining why standard box-average generateMipmap() on a depth buffer causes 94.12% false occlusion popping through doorways and fences
  • Reducing each 2×2 Hi-Z mip level with conservative farthest-depth (max in linear Z, min in Reversed-Z)
  • Replacing 64-pixel full-resolution bounding-box depth scans with a single O(1) Hi-Z mip lookup

⛔ Not for

  • View-frustum-only culling where depth buffer occlusion is never tested (use Gribb-Hartmann frustum planes instead)
  • Color texture mipmapping where box/Kaiser averaging is desired for anti-aliasing
  • Neural potentially-visible-set (PVS) classification networks
  • Translucent glass windows that do not write to the occluder depth buffer
