MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time Eulerian smoke plumes, fire, cloud billow simulation, and velocity self-advection (Dupont & Liu 2003 / Selle et al. 2008)
  • GPU compute-shader fluid solvers where hardware bilinear texture lookups are cheap and second-order accuracy is needed without wide cubic stencils
  • Level-set and signed-distance-field advection requiring minimal curvature dissipation
  • Algebraic round-trip error subtraction replacing 64-mode spectral Fourier advection

⛔ Not for

  • Sharp discontinuous step edges without min/max limiter clamping (always clamp MacCormack output to the local 2x2 donor cell bounds to prevent Gibbs ringing)
  • Static non-advecting textures
  • 1D scalar UI sliders
  • Rigid-body translation
