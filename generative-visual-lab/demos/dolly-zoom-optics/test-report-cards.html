MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Vertigo / Hitchcock dolly-zoom shots with locked foreground scale
  • Coupling camera intrinsics (FOV) and extrinsics (distance) analytically
  • Pre-computing exact focal-length schedules for Generative Photography
  • Explaining perspective compression without subject size drift

⛔ Not for

  • Generative Photography text-to-image diffusion synthesis
  • Physical aperture circle-of-confusion bokeh rendering
  • Anamorphic lens barrel distortion calibration
  • Motorized cinema zoom-lens backlash compensation
