MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Screen-Space Subsurface Scattering (SSSSS), Gaussian bloom, and bilateral depth-aware blurs in UE5, Unity HDRP, and Call of Duty
  • Chaining Pass 2 (Vertical 1D) onto Pass 1's intermediate render target (Horizontal 1D) to synthesize the full N*N outer product in 2N taps
  • Capturing the u^2 diagonal quadrant mass (0.2500 at u=0.50, 0.3600 at u=0.60, 0.6400 at u=0.80) missed by single-pass + cross filters
  • Cutting texture fetches from N^2 (64 taps) to 2N (16 taps) with zero mathematical loss on rank-1 kernels

⛔ Not for

  • Highly anisotropic Curved-Motion-Blur trajectories that do not align with orthogonal screen axes
  • Unfiltered 1-tap point lookups (u = 0)
  • Sparse geometry vertex shaders
  • 1D audio equalizers
