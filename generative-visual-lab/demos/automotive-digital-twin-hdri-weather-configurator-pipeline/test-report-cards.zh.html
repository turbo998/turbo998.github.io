MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Global automotive launch TVCs, 30-country dealer localized road campaigns (LHD/RHD + regional plates), and online WebGPU 3D car configurators
  • Coupling OEM Class-A CAD vehicles and measured dual-layer Clearcoat PBR shaders with 360-degree dynamic HDRI domes extracted from AI-generated environments
  • Adding velocity-matched tire water spray, snow tracks, desert dust wakes, and Matrix ADB headlight road-carpet projections

⛔ 不适合

  • Feeding a static car photo into unconstrained Image-to-Video diffusion where wheels morph spokes, brake calipers spin, and panel gaps bend
  • Pasting an unlit static car cutout over a moving background without dynamic HDRI clearcoat reflections
  • Low-poly cartoon racing games that do not require OEM CAD accuracy
