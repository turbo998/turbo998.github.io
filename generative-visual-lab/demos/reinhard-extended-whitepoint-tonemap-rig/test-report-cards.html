MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • HDR-to-SDR tone reproduction with a calibrated finite scene white point L_white (Reinhard et al. SIGGRAPH 2002 Eq. 4)
  • Real-time PBR viewports, emissive UI badges, and specular glints that must reach crisp 1.0000 display white without hard clamp clipping
  • Dynamic exposure adaptation where L_white tracks the 99th-percentile scene luminance
  • 1-FMA numerator correction replacing 64-substep Weber-Fechner ODE integration

⛔ Not for

  • Chromaticity-preserving gamut mapping without per-channel inset/outset matrices (use AgX for extreme saturated neon)
  • Linear radiometric G-buffer storage prior to post-processing
  • Unlit 1-bit monochrome icons
  • 1D audio limiter DSP
