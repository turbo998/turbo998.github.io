MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D fountain mushroom domes, impinging water-jet splashes, spoon/plate sink water-bell sheets, and rocket impinging injector sprays
  • Computing the exact Taylor-Culick sonic rim termination radius R_bell = rho * Q * v_0 / (4 * pi * sigma) where h(R) = 2*sigma / (rho * v_0^2)
  • Coupling water-bell expansion directly to jet Weber number We_jet = rho * v_0^2 * R_jet / (2 * sigma)

⛔ Not for

  • Dry sand or gravel impacts with zero surface tension (sigma = 0)
  • Low-speed dripping faucets that never form a continuous radial sheet (We < 1)
  • Static 2D UI text
