MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Forearm pronation/supination wrist twists, shoulder rolls, and articulated character rigging up to 175 deg
  • Explaining why Euclidean matrix averaging (1-w)R0 + wR1 scales radius by cos(theta/2) into a candy-wrapper pinch
  • Direct vector cross-product dual-quaternion skinning inside WebGL2 / WebGPU vertex shaders without 4x4 matrix unpacks
  • Skeletal driving of 3D Gaussian Splatting avatars (GART / GauHuman / RigAnything) where volume collapse breaks splat density

⛔ Not for

  • Pure translational sliding joints without rotation where LBS and DQS are identical
  • Extreme 180-degree antipodal singular twists without intermediate twist-bone decomposition
  • Secondary muscle bulging or cloth wrinkling that requires pose-space deformation (PSD) or finite-element simulation
  • Uncalibrated monocular video-to-video diffusion transfer without skeletal bone weights
