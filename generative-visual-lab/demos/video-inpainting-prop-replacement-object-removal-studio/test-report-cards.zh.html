MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Erasing photobombing passersby, boom mics, wire rigs, and competitor billboards from moving-camera commercial footage
  • Replacing legacy product packaging (2024 bottle/phone) in archive TVCs with the 2026 new SKU while preserving actor hand-grip finger occlusion
  • Combining ProPainter bidirectional optical-flow pixel propagation (85% real background reuse) with DiffuEraser / Wan-VACE 3D latent-tube synthesis

⛔ 不适合

  • Running single-image 2D LaMa or SD Inpainting frame-by-frame on video (creates boiling "Predator cloak" water-ripple shimmer)
  • Omitting cast shadows from the object removal mask (leaves a dark ghost shadow walking across the floor)
  • Audio-only podcast editing
