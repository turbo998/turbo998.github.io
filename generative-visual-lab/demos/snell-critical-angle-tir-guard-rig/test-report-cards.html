MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Underwater upward-looking camera shaders ("Snell's Window" beyond the critical angle)
  • Diamond, crystal, and glass back-face internal reflections where relative IOR eta = n_1 / n_2 > 1.0
  • Dielectric path tracers and screen-space refraction shaders preventing IEEE-754 sqrt(-u) NaN bloom fireflies
  • Schlick Fresnel using transmitted cosine cosThetaT = sqrt(max(0, k)) so R = 1.0000 automatically when k <= 0

⛔ Not for

  • Opaque metallic conductors without dielectric transmission
  • Pure diffuse Lambertian clay surfaces
  • 2D flat UI typography
  • 1D audio volume sliders
