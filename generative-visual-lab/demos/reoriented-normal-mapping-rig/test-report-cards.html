MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Detail normal map blending (skin pores, micro-scratches, terrain rock detail, water ripple overlays) in UE5, Unity HDRP, and Three.js
  • Explaining why naive UDN XY addition flattens combined normals by −15.79° at 74° and up to −21.53° at 82°
  • Rotating tangent-space detail normal n₂ onto macro base normal n₁ in 6 ALU ops without trigonometric calls or 3×3 matrices
  • Preserving unit normal length automatically when both input normals are unit vectors

⛔ Not for

  • Single-layer normal mapped meshes without secondary detail normal overlays
  • Heightfield bump addition where raw scalar heights h₁(u,v) + h₂(u,v) are summed prior to finite differencing
  • Neural texture synthesis or diffusion normal map generation models
  • Unlit flat-shaded polygons without normal mapping
