MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Rough matte materials (terracotta, dry earth, moon dust, chalk, unglazed ceramics, felt) in Disney Principled BRDF, Frostbite, and Unity HDRP
  • Explaining why constant Lambertian diffuse loses -78.58% (-3.6685x) of the grazing retroreflective rim on rough surfaces
  • Coupling diffuse grazing reflectance F_D90 = 0.5 + 2·r·cos²θ_d directly to the specular roughness parameter r
  • Evaluating (1 - μ)^5 via a 3-multiply register chain instead of transcendental pow(1 - μ, 5.0)

⛔ Not for

  • Pure metallic conductors (metallic = 1.0) where diffuse reflectance ρ is zero
  • Mirror-smooth dielectrics where specular Fresnel dominates the grazing rim
  • Neural SVBRDF capture or photo-to-material diffusion models
  • Subsurface-dominated translucent wax or jade where deep volumetric scattering dominates surface V-cavities
