MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Gold, copper, brass, aluminum, and chrome metals in MaterialX artistic_ior, OpenPBR, Arnold, and Three.js
  • Preserving the Pseudo-Brewster reflectance dip at cos(theta_B) = sqrt(eta^2 + kappa^2) (60° dip to 0.2500)
  • Eliminating the +48.65% chalky edge whitewash of monotonic dielectric Schlick approximations on metals
  • Evaluating exact complex-IOR reflectance in 5 scalar operations without complex square roots

⛔ Not for

  • Pure dielectrics with zero extinction (kappa == 0) such as water or glass where standard Schlick is accurate
  • Unlit matte clay or diffuse chalk
  • Multi-layer thin-film interference coatings (use Belcour-Barla Iridescence Round 102)
  • Flat 2D vector icons
