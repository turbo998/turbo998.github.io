MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Upsampling half-resolution volumetric fog, god-rays, clouds, and SSAO to full resolution in UE5, Frostbite, and Unity HDRP
  • Explaining why hardware bilinear filtering across a 2.0m → 25.0m depth cliff leaks +0.4500 (10.00x) glowing fog halos onto dark foreground characters
  • Using full-resolution G-buffer depth range weights exp(-Δz^2 / 2σ_z^2) to reject cross-silhouette low-res taps
  • Replacing full-resolution 64-step volumetric ray marching with half-res shading + 2-tap Joint Bilateral Upsampling

⛔ Not for

  • Full-resolution passes that do not use lower-resolution intermediate render targets
  • Pure 2D UI image scaling where no 3D G-buffer depth guide texture exists
  • Neural single-image depth estimation or AI inpainting networks
  • Sub-pixel thin hair strands narrower than 1 full-res pixel without MSAA or deep opacity maps
