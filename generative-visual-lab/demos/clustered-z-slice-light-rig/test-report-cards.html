MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Clustered Forward+ and Clustered Deferred 3D froxel light culling grids (DOOM idTech, Three.js WebGPU, Godot 4, Unreal Strata)
  • Explaining why linear Z-slicing (Δz = 58.82m) dumps 12/17 near-to-mid depth bands into Slice 0 while leaving 11 slices empty
  • Maintaining cubical self-similar 3D froxels (Δz/z = const) across a 10,000:1 perspective depth range
  • Evaluating slice indices in O(1) via floor(ln(z) * scale + bias) using two precomputed camera uniforms

⛔ Not for

  • Orthographic 2D side-scrollers where frustum width does not expand with depth z
  • Single-directional-light outdoor scenes without point or spot light culling grids
  • Neural light importance sampling or ReSTIR reservoir resampling networks
  • Baked static lightmaps with zero dynamic runtime point lights
