This bench evaluates deterministic Cem Yuksel, Scott Schaefer & John Keyser (CAD 2011) centripetal Catmull-Rom spline parameterization (α = 0.5, Δt_k = ∥P_{k+1} − P_k∥^0.5) via precomputed Horner cubic polynomials versus naive uniform Catmull-Rom (α = 0.0) and recursive 3-level Barry-Goldman knot pyramids across 17 stations on a 2.0 m short bridge span flanked by 16.12 m approach chords.
