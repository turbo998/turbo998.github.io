MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Digital human hair, animal fur, and keratin fibers in UE5 Groom, Blender Principled Hair BSDF, and RenderMan
  • Separating the sharp white surface glint (R at -6.0°) from the warm melanin-colored internal glint (TRT at +8.0°)
  • Eliminating the single unshifted 0.0° plastic wig band and -99.25% TRT starvation of Kajiya-Kay shaders
  • Evaluating dual longitudinal Gaussians in 6 scalar operations without per-ray cylinder tracing

⛔ Not for

  • Synthetic cylindrical nylon wires lacking tilted imbricated cuticle scales (alpha_c == 0°)
  • Flat isotropic plastic or matte masonry
  • Upright velvet cloth pile (use Estevez-Kulla Charlie Sheen Round 103)
  • Unlit billboard hair cards without tangent vectors
