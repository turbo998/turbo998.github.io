MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Vertex wind-sway shaders for tree branches, grass blades, and foliage bending around wind axis k without rubber-band stretching
  • Rigid-body orientation updates, robotic arm kinematics, and camera turret rotation on SO(3)
  • Exact centripetal pullback -v_0*(1 - cos theta) eliminating Pythagorean tangent drift
  • Closed-form Rodrigues evaluation replacing 64-substep Cayley Lie-group integration

⛔ 不适合

  • Intentional non-rigid squash-and-stretch cartoon deformations
  • Purely translational particle advection
  • Static 2D UI layouts
  • 1D scalar audio envelopes
