MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Subsurface scattering wrap lighting for skin, wax, clouds, and foliage (Green GPU Gems 2004 / UE5 / Unity HDRP / Frostbite)
  • Soft area-light wrap approximations that must not inflate total scene luminance when artists widen wrap parameter w
  • 自动放行mated material LODs transitioning between standard Lambert (w=0) and translucent wrap (w>0) with zero exposure jump
  • Closed-form 1/(1+w)^2 normalization replacing 64-ring spherical cap quadrature

⛔ 不适合

  • Intentional non-physical stylized rim boosts where artists want total brightness to double
  • Replacing shadow maps for hard cast occluders
  • Unlit 2D UI elements
  • 1D audio compression
