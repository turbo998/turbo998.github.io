MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Real-time cloth, rope, hair, and soft-tissue tendons across variable display refresh rates (30/60/120/240 Hz) and substep counts
  • Explaining why unscaled classic PBD shrinks extension as 1/N_s² (−93.75% stiffening at 4 substeps) while explicit penalty springs explode
  • Mapping real engineering Youngs modulus / Hooke compliance α = 1/k directly into position-based solvers via α̃ = α / Δt_s²
  • Using small-step XPBD sub-stepping to converge in a single pass instead of running 48-iteration Gauss-Seidel loops

⛔ Not for

  • Unconstrained ballistic projectiles in free flight without distance, bending, or volume constraints
  • Static rigid-body props that never deform or collide
  • Neural cloth surrogate networks or learned mesh graph simulators
  • Purely kinematic keyframe animations without physical forces
