MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Automotive metallic basecoat + polyurethane clearcoat, lacquered wood, carbon fiber, and wet surfaces in glTF KHR_materials_clearcoat and Three.js
  • Attenuating substrate diffuse and metallic flakes by (1 - F_coat) so total reflectance never exceeds 1.0000
  • Recomputing internal substrate Fresnel from relative IOR (n_sub / n_coat) to capture wet-surface darkening
  • Evaluating Schlick clearcoat partition in 5 scalar multiplications without multi-bounce loops

⛔ Not for

  • Uncoated single-layer matte drywall or raw concrete
  • Nanometer soap bubbles where wave phase interference dominates (use Belcour-Barla Iridescence Round 102)
  • Upright velvet cloth pile (use Estevez-Kulla Charlie Sheen Round 103)
  • Unlit 2D UI icons
