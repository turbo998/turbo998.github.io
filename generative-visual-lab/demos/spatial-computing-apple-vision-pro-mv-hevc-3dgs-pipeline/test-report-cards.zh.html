MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Converting 2D 4K brand commercials and AIGC videos into Apple Vision Pro Spatial Video (MV-HEVC with vexu/hfov/cdat atoms) and Quest 3 WebXR streams
  • Clamping binocular parallax within [-1.8%, +2.2%] FOV and inpainting disoccluded background strips via Layered Depth Images (LDI)
  • Pairing spatial narrative video with interactive RealityKit USDZ / Draco GLB 3D hero models in VIP retail showrooms

⛔ 不适合

  • Unclamped close-up negative parallax (>3% FOV) that forces severe eye convergence and immediate HMD nausea
  • Frame-by-frame monocular depth normalization without temporal consistency locking
  • 2D-only print newspaper layouts
