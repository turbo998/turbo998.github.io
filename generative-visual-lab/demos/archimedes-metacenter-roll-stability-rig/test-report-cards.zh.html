MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • 3D ocean ships, wide-beam cargo barges, catamarans, floating icebergs, and buoyant crate roll-oscillation animations
  • Evaluating metacentric radius BM = I_wp / V = B^2 / (12 * draft) so hulls with center of gravity G above static center of buoyancy B_0 remain upright whenever M lies above G (GM > 0)
  • Driving natural ship roll period T_roll = 2*pi * k_xx / sqrt(g * GM)

⛔ 不适合

  • Fully submerged submarines where waterplane area I_wp = 0 and BM = 0 (requiring B strictly above G)
  • Land vehicles on dry asphalt
  • Static 2D menus
