MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 24/7 digital-twin dashboards, shader wave oscillators, and particle emitters running for hours or days
  • Explaining why IEEE-754 float32 swallows any increment Delta < 0.5 * 2^(floor(log2(T)) - 23)
  • Wrapping periodic shader phase uniforms into [0, 2*pi) on the CPU with Kahan error compensation before GPU upload
  • Camera-relative origin rebasing for large-scale 3D/4D Gaussian Splatting city and planetary worlds

⛔ Not for

  • Short 5-second one-shot UI transitions starting at t = 0 where float32 ULP is 1e-7
  • Cryptographic hash or financial ledger balances requiring exact decimal integer cents
  • Long-horizon autoregressive video diffusion memory banks (WorldMem / FramePack)
  • Hardware double-precision (float64) compute kernels where 53 mantissa bits never saturate within human lifetimes
