MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Per-fragment tangent-space normal mapping on curved meshes and skinned characters
  • Anisotropic GGX brushed metal and hair shading requiring mutually orthogonal tangent/bitangent axes
  • Clearcoat bottom-normal and detail normal map blending across UV seams and high-curvature fillets
  • Single-projection T_orth = normalize(T - dot(N, T) * N) before B = cross(N, T_orth) * tangent.w

⛔ Not for

  • Un-interpolated flat face normals where vertex tangents are constant per triangle
  • Static 2D UI layout
  • Omnidirectional point-light attenuation without surface normals
  • 1D audio gain sliders
