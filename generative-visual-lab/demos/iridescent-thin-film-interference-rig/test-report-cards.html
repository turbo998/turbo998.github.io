MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Soap bubbles, scarab beetle shells, anodized titanium, mother-of-pearl, and anti-reflective lens coatings in glTF KHR_materials_iridescence, MaterialX, Blender, and Three.js
  • Bounding grazing-angle optical path difference inside the Snell critical cone (cos(theta_t) >= sqrt(1 - 1/n^2) = 0.7454 for n=1.50)
  • Preventing the -38.76% at 60° and -76.94% at 80° false multi-order rainbow cycling of unrefracted cos(theta_i) shaders
  • Evaluating exact refracted cosine from dot(N, V) in 3 algebraic operations plus 1 square root without inverse trig

⛔ Not for

  • Non-iridescent opaque matte paints or uncoated bulk metals without a dielectric film layer
  • Diffraction gratings (CD/DVD tracks, holographic foil) governed by surface groove pitch d*sin(theta) rather than thin-film thickness
  • Cholesteric liquid crystals with helical Bragg pitch rotation
  • Unlit 2D UI gradients
