MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Soap bubble bursting animations, crown-splash liquid sheet perforation, waterfall curtain tearing, and viscous slime rupture VFX
  • Scaling hole expansion velocity inversely with square root of local film thickness v_TC = sqrt(2*sigma/(rho*h)) so gravity-drained thin bubble tops zip open faster than thick bottom pools
  • Sizing the toroidal rim bead volume V_rim = int h(r) 2*pi*r dr from swept film mass conservation

⛔ 不适合

  • Brittle glass shattering where crack propagation is governed by Rayleigh elastic wave speed rather than capillary tension
  • Rigid opaque stone walls
  • Static 2D icons
