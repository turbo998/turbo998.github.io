MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Cinematic camera pans, robotic arm turrets, and hero character joints in Three.js, Unreal Engine, Blender, and glTF animations
  • Maintaining exact constant angular velocity V_Slerp(u) = 1.0000x along wide S^3 geodesic rotation arcs
  • Eliminating Nlerp's 1.6000x (u=0.25/0.75) and 2.0000x (+100% midpoint u=0.50) angular velocity whip-pan surge
  • Replacing 64-substep Riemannian ODE integration with closed-form trigonometric/Chebyshev Slerp

⛔ Not for

  • Tiny sub-degree micro-rotations (Omega < 1 deg) where chord radius r(u)^2 ~ 1.0 and Nlerp is indistinguishable
  • Multi-keyframe C1 spline transitions requiring cubic tangent control (use Logarithmic Quaternion Squad Round 60)
  • Dual-quaternion skinning weight blending across a single frame (use DQS Round 30)
  • Purely translational 3D positions
