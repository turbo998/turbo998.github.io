MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Froxel volumetric fog, horizon cumulus cloud raymarching, and OpenVDB fire/explosion shaders (Max 1995 / Hillaire SIGGRAPH 2016)
  • Coarse LOD raymarching steps at far distances where optical depth per step tau = sigma_t * ds exceeds 1.0
  • Blackbody thermal emission and single-scattering integration where T + (1 - T) === 1.0 guarantees White Furnace conservation
  • 1-subtraction (1 - T) reuse of exp(-tau) replacing 64-substep RTE micro-marching

⛔ Not for

  • Pure vacuum raycasting with zero participating media (sigma_t === 0)
  • Opaque surface-only deferred shading
  • 2D flat UI text rendering
  • 1D audio gain sliders
