MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise e-commerce 1000-SKU promotional video factories, multi-locale social ad pipelines, and personalized annual user recap videos
  • Combining cached AI generative B-roll / IC-Light product relighting (Wan 2.2, Kling 2.1, Veo 3, Sora 2) with Remotion deterministic React/SVG/Canvas overlays
  • Guaranteeing 100% accurate SKU packaging typography, real-time database price binding, frame-exact TTS 为什么企业电商千款 SKU 短视频与全球多语种营销投放绝不能直接用纯 AI 视频大模型（Sora / Veo / Kling / Wan）“端到端硬画价格与包装文字”，而必须采用“AI 写实环境镜头缓存 + Remotion 确定性代码图层合成”混合架构？ sync, and C2PA cryptographic watermarks

⛔ 不适合

  • Single-shot experimental art films with zero text, logo, or SKU consistency constraints
  • Offline fluid/structural engineering CFD
  • Unauthenticated deepfake generation without C2PA provenance
