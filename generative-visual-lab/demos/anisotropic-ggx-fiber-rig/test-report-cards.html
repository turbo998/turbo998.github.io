MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Brushed stainless steel, spun aluminum wheels, vinyl records, silk satin fabrics, and combed hair strands in UE5, Unity HDRP, and Three.js
  • Stretching specular highlights into a continuous transverse ribbon (I=0.6400 at u=+0.40) perpendicular to micro-grooves
  • Coupling with MikkTSpace Orthogonal Tangent Frames (Round 62) and Heitz Smith Height-CorrelatedAnisotropic Masking-Shadowing
  • Evaluating slope-space elliptical roughness (alpha_t, alpha_b) in 4 algebraic operations without trig or facet loops

⛔ Not for

  • Purely isotropic sandblasted matte surfaces or uniform glass spheres where alpha_t == alpha_b
  • Meshes lacking valid tangent/bitangent attributes or derivative-based screen-space tangent frames
  • Multi-bounce volumetric hair transmission without Marschner TT/TRT longitudinal lobes
  • Unlit flat-shaded cartoon decals
