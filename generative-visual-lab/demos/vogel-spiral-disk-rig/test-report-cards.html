MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Circular camera Depth-of-Field (DoF) bokeh gather kernels, PCSS soft-shadow blocker/PCF search disks, and screen-space ambient occlusion
  • Explaining why naive polar sampling r = (k+0.5)/N crams 4.0000× too many samples into the center core while starving the outer rim by -50.00%
  • Restoring exact 1.0000× equi-area Voronoi cell density via the inverse-CDF Jacobian square-root mapping r_k = sqrt((k+0.5)/N)
  • Replacing per-tap transcendental cos/sin calls with a 2D complex-rotor recurrence rotated by the golden angle 137.5078°

⛔ Not for

  • 1D separable Gaussian box blurs where horizontal and vertical passes decouple completely
  • Polygonal diaphragm bokeh with sharp hexagonal/octagonal straight blades requiring polygon-edge warping
  • Neural depth-of-field bokeh rendering or diffusion-based defocus models
  • Pinhole cameras with zero aperture radius (infinite depth of field)
