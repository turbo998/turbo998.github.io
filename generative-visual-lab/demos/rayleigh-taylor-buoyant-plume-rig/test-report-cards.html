MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D underwater ink drops, volcanic mushroom clouds, oil-water lava lamps, and supernova remnant Rayleigh-Taylor finger animations
  • Dividing net buoyancy (rho_2 - rho_1)*g by coupled two-fluid inertia (rho_2 + rho_1) via the exact Atwood number A = (rho_2 - rho_1)/(rho_2 + rho_1)
  • Preventing unphysical supersonic acceleration (> g) when heavy-to-light density ratio rho_2 / rho_1 is large

⛔ Not for

  • Single-phase compressible acoustic sound waves with zero gravity
  • Rigid mechanical gear trains
  • Static 2D UI text
