MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • PBR microfacet shaders in Frostbite, Unreal Engine 5, Filament, Unity HDRP, and Three.js (V_GGX_SmithCorrelated)
  • Algebraically canceling 2*mu_v*mu_l in G_2 against the 4*mu_v*mu_l Cook-Torrance denominator before division
  • Guaranteeing a finite horizon limit V(0.00) = 0.5000 without ad-hoc max(NdotV, 1e-4) clamping or FP16 NaN rings
  • Saving 2 multiplies and 1 division per light evaluation while eliminating grazing silhouette firefly rings

⛔ Not for

  • Legacy Phong or Blinn-Phong shaders that lack a microfacet Jacobian denominator
  • Unlit flat color materials
  • Purely diffuse Lambertian shading
  • 2D post-processing filters
