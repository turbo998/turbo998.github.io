MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Swept 3D ribbons, extruded cables, motion-graphics trails, and camera rails crossing S-curve inflection points
  • Explaining why Frenet-Serret frames N = r''/||r''|| snap 180 degrees whenever curvature changes sign
  • Trig-free algebraic dihedral reflection transport without acos/sin/cos or 3x3 matrix allocations
  • Orienting 4D Gaussian Splatting trajectory tubes and procedural hair/vine meshes without bowtie twisting

⛔ Not for

  • Aircraft coordinated roll banking where roll angle must scale proportionally with centripetal acceleration v^2*kappa
  • Closed periodic loops with nonzero Gauss-Bonnet holonomy angle without an end-to-end twist distributing pass
  • Uncalibrated monocular video camera pose estimation (COLMAP / MegaSaM)
  • Neural video trajectory diffusion inference (Gen3C / TrajectoryCrafter)
