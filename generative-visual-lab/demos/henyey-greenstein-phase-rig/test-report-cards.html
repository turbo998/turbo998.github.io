MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Single and multiple scattering in volumetric clouds, god-rays, and underwater media (Horizon Nubis, UE5 Volumetric Cloud, Flight Simulator)
  • Proving why the (1 - g^2) numerator is mandatory to cancel the 1/(1 - g^2) integral growth of the (1 + g^2 - 2g*mu)^(-3/2) forward lobe
  • Blending dual-lobe Henyey-Greenstein (forward g_1 = +0.80 silver-lining + backward g_2 = -0.30 glory) while preserving unit total energy
  • Replacing 64-subinterval spherical phase integration with closed-form O(1) normalized lobe evaluation

⛔ Not for

  • Pure Rayleigh molecular sky scattering where the symmetric 3/4*(1 + cos^2 θ) phase function applies
  • Opaque surface BRDFs without participating media or subsurface volumetric scattering
  • Neural cloud relighting or 2D skybox style-transfer networks
  • Non-scattering pure absorption filters (scattering albedo ω_0 = 0)
