MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Enterprise executive roundtables, customer case-study interviews, tech video podcasts, and webinar recordings (multi-cam or 4K virtual multi-crop)
  • Combining multichannel mic VAD with TalkNet visual lip-motion correlation and a 1.8s minimum shot-hold hysteresis gate to ignore coughs, laughs, and <1.2s backchannels
  • Exporting non-destructive Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve FCPXML timelines alongside automated 9:16 vertical social highlight reels

⛔ 不适合

  • Switching cameras instantaneously on raw dB audio spikes without minimum hold hysteresis or lip-motion verification
  • Leaving a 60-minute 3-guest interview on a single static wide shot with zero close-ups or B-roll cutaways
  • Unvoiced instrumental music videos
