MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Sub-pixel image scaling, TAA history reprojection, ray-traced film reconstruction, and font/UI resampling
  • Explaining why filters must satisfy Partition of Unity sum(w_m(s)) == 1 to prevent flat-field checkerboard ripples
  • Selecting B=1/3, C=1/3 on the B+2C=1 locus to balance blur against negative-lobe Gibbs ringing halos
  • Branchless Horner-factored 4-tap polynomial evaluation without per-tap Math.abs or if/else branching

⛔ Not for

  • Strictly non-negative probability density estimation where even a -0.0347 negative lobe is forbidden (use B=1, C=0 B-spline)
  • Exact interpolation of discrete keyframe knots where k(0)=1 is mandatory (use Fritsch-Carlson PCHIP or B=0, C=0.5)
  • Generative hallucination of missing high-frequency textures (DLSS / StableSR)
  • 1-tap nearest-neighbor pixel-art magnification where crisp blocky texel boundaries are desired
