MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • 3D viewport gizmos, motion trails, spline ribbons, and debug wireframes that cross the camera near plane (Sutherland & Hodgman 1974 / Blinn & Newell 1978)
  • Software/compute-shader rasterizers, meshlet cullers, and SVG/Canvas2D 3D line projectors
  • Screen-space portal clipping and decals where one endpoint moves behind or close to the camera pinhole w <= w_near
  • 1-divide linear homogeneous interpolation replacing 64-step ray-plane bisection

⛔ Not for

  • 2D orthographic HUD overlays where w === 1.0 is constant everywhere
  • Static background skyboxes evaluated purely by view direction
  • 1D audio timelines
  • Fixed-screen-space post-processing fullscreen quads
