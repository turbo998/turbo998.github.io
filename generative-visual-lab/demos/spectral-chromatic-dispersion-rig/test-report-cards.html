MY TAKE

Exact controls are useful; realism remains a separate job.

✅ Good fit

  • Cinematic radial chromatic aberration and barrel lens dispersion in Playdead INSIDE, UE5 PostProcess, and Unity HDRP
  • Explaining why 3-tap RGB channel splitting breaks thin specular highlights into 3 disconnected Red/Green/Blue ghosts with 6 dead-black gaps
  • Accumulating jittered multi-tap samples weighted by a normalized spectral LUT ∑ w_λ(t_k) = (1, 1, 1)
  • Replacing 64-wavelength brute-force quadrature with closed-form spectral overlap integration

⛔ Not for

  • Zero-dispersion pinhole camera renders where all wavelengths focus at identical pixel coordinates
  • Deliberate retro 1980s VHS / anaglyph 3D-glasses glitch effects where harsh RGB separation is an artistic goal
  • Neural bokeh or camera-lens style-transfer networks
  • Text-heavy HUD interfaces that should bypass post-process lens distortion entirely
