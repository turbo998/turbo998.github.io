MY TAKE · 选型边界

精确控制有用；逼真外观是另一项工作。

✅ 适合

  • Close-up water raindrop ripples, puddle splashes, teacup menisci, and insect-wake capillary waves (lambda <= 5 cm)
  • Unified multi-band FFT ocean and pond spectra transitioning smoothly from gravity swells to sub-centimeter capillary ripples
  • Correcting anomalous high-frequency group velocity where capillary ripples outrun gravity crests
  • Closed-form shader evaluation omega(k) = sqrt((g*k + (gamma/rho)*k^3) * tanh(k*h))

⛔ 不适合

  • Breaking overturning plunge surf requiring full 3D Navier-Stokes / SPH particle solvers
  • Static 2D flat UI cards
  • Rigid-body dry box collisions
  • 1D audio volume sliders
