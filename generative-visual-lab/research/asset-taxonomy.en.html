# Generative Visual Lab · SA Architecture & Technical Asset Taxonomy

> 🌐 **Language / 语言**: [中文 (`asset-taxonomy.md`)](asset-taxonomy.md) | **English**

Written from a **Cloud & AI Solution Architect (SA)** perspective, this taxonomy catalogs all **260 interactive visual demonstrations, Blender 4.5 LTS MCP asset pipelines, scientific datasets, bilingual architecture decision guides, and automated verification gates** in `turbo998/generative-visual-lab`. All legacy classical-mechanics and sensor-parameter formula benches have been removed so every asset serves generative visual engineering, controllable multimodal media, 3D/4D world models, or enterprise AIGC production architecture.
