# Overnight Research Cartoon Shorts · Storyboard (`painted-animation` 16:9)

Built with the Opus 5.5 `painted-animation` skill (`p5.js` + `p5.brush` + `clawd.js` + `karaoke.js`).
All 6 episodes are **16:9 (`1920×1080`, 24 fps) hand-painted watercolor & boiling-ink cartoon knowledge shorts** with zero portrait (`9:16`) videos and zero PPT/dashboard cards. Every concept is acted out by **Clawd** and custom painted cartoon characters/props, accompanied by spoken Chinese narration and a boiling-ink karaoke subtitle lozenge with visual strike-through gags (`pun`).
