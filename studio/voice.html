VOICE & SPEECH · VOXCPM2 VS. AZURE SPEECHARCHITECTURE

VoxCPM2 vs. Azure Speech: Acoustic Architecture & TTS Benchmark ↗

11-WAV 48 kHz Welch FFT (17.7 kHz BW) · Internal FSQ(512, s=9) 50.88 dB Whisper SDR · vLLM-Omni RTF 0.13 · TCO Break-Even @ 18M–25M chars/mo

Enterprise speech workload routing across expressive multilingual dubbing and regulated IVR/avatar channels: auditing Tsinghua OpenBMB VoxCPM2 (2B, arXiv:2606.06928, internal FSQ(512, s=9) + unquantized residual -> LocDiT 10-step flow -> 48 kHz AudioVAE V2) against Microsoft Azure AI Speech (DragonHDOmni / Personal Voice, deterministic SSML, 0.1 ms viseme sync, mandatory audio watermark, 99.9% SLA), backed by 11-WAV 1024-pt Welch FFT forensics (16,781–17,656 Hz effective bandwidth; +14.6 dB INT8 quantization noise floor) and an interactive TCO break-even calculator ($3.2k vs $12.8k/mo at 850M chars/mo).
