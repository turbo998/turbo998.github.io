06 · HYBRID ROUTING PLAYBOOK & ANTI-DEEPFAKE GOVERNANCE

Enterprise Hybrid Speech Routing Playbook & Anti-Spoofing Controls

1. Workload Routing Criteria

  • Route banking IVR, healthcare advisories, and strict heteronym/currency readouts 100% to Microsoft Azure AI Speech with SSML.
  • Route real-time 3D avatar lip-sync workloads to Azure Speech synchronous 0.1 ms Viseme & blendshape streams.
  • Route high-volume audiobooks, podcasts, expressive NPCs, and SEA localization (>25M chars/mo) to self-hosted VoxCPM2 on vLLM-Omni.

2. Streaming Sentinel & Front-End TN

  • Place a deterministic Text Normalizer (WeTextProcessing / NeMo TN) ahead of VoxCPM2 to expand numbers, dates, and currency symbols.
  • Compensate for voxcpm2.py:667 disabling retry_badcase in streaming mode by enforcing a real-time patch_to_char_ratio circuit breaker with instant failover to Azure Speech.
  • Wrap offline Voice Design and dense scripts in an automated ASR verification gate (re-roll up to 2 seeds if CER > 2%).

3. Anti-Spoofing & Provenance Mandate

  • Deprecate voice-only biometric authentication across wire approvals and IT resets; enforce FIDO2/Passkey or out-of-band cryptographic signing.
  • Inject mandatory AudioSeal frame-level invisible watermarks + C2PA provenance manifests on every self-hosted VoxCPM2 output stream.
  • Require dynamic live consent-phrase verification before enrolling any reference voice, and deploy WavLM + AASIST3 deepfake detectors on SIP ingress.
