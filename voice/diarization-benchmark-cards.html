7 / 7 · MY TAKE GOOD FIT VS. NOT FOR & READINESS

Architectural Guidance: Combine Stereo Channel Baseline with Explicit Diarization Gate

Production engineering recommendations from the Azure Speech Fast Transcription Diarization PoC.

✅ Good Fit (Recommended)

• Post-Call QA & Dual-Recording Audits: Synchronous Fast Transcription at 0.02–0.06 RTF processes 3–4 minute calls in 4–13 seconds.

• Mono Mixed Recordings with Unknown Locale: Pair explicit diarization.enabled = True with --locale auto and maxSpeakers=2..4.

• Split-Channel Stereo Contact Centers: Retain Channel Transcription (active_channels=[0,1]) as deterministic ground truth and cross-check against mono diarization.

• Zero-Key Enterprise Security: Provision Speech S0 with disableLocalAuth: true and resource-scoped DefaultAzureCredential.

⛔ Not For (Anti-Patterns)

• Relying on Default maxSpeakers in SDK 1.0.0: Omitting diarization.enabled = True silently drops all speaker labels (0/8).

• Hardcoding en-US on Multilingual/Spanish Queues: Causes severe confidence degradation when callers speak es-ES.

• Treating Silent/Sparse Audio as Diarization Pass: Files with 0 phrases (file-03/04) or <0.50 confidence (file-05) must route to manual review.

• Sub-200ms Live Full-Duplex Interruption: Fast Transcription is for synchronous file/chunk transcription; use GPT-Live-1 / Voice Live for real-time S2S barge-in.
